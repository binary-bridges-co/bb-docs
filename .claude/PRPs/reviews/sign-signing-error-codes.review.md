## Verdict

REJECTED (round 1). The factual base is mostly sound: production (bb-infrastructure origin/main, edge-api.yaml:29) runs v0.37.0, sets no `APP_SIGN_*` or `SIGNATURE_IMAGE_*` variable, and runs `replicas: 1` (edge-api.yaml:18), so the application.yml defaults are what production uses today; every application.yml line cited in R4 (242, 245, 247, 250, 251, 259) is exact. But the plan would make the page over-promise in three places the owner named: a `RATE_LIMITED` that always means "wait minutes", a `SIGNATURE_UPLOAD_TIMEOUT` that always reaches the client, and "every refusal leaves the link unused" backed by a source that does not say it. Each needs a research fix and a wording constraint in the tasks before implementation.
