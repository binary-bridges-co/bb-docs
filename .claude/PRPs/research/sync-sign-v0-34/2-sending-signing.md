# Audit 2: sending, signing, getting-started, sign/index (bb-docs vs bb-sign main)

Docs: `bb-docs/.claude/worktrees/sync-sign-v0-34` (92077ba). Ground truth: bb-sign `audit-main` (df6a2a6, v0.34.0 + v0.34.1 frontend), bb-infrastructure `audit-main` (5a8468f; all services `v0.34.0`, FrontendVersion v0.34.1).

Path shorthands: `es:` = `src/content/docs/sign/`, `en:` = `src/content/docs/en/sign/`. `FE` = `bb-sign/frontend/src`, `EA` = `bb-sign/edge-api/src/main/kotlin/co/binarybridges/sign/edge`, `NS` = `bb-sign/notification-service/src/main`, `INFRA` = `bb-infrastructure`.

## What production does today (this shapes every finding)

1. **Archived envelopes stay downloadable.** The nightly reaper still moves closed envelopes out at 30 days (`edge-api application.yml:205`, prod sets no override). They now open a read-only detail page with their downloads and audit trail (#245). A signer's link keeps serving the signed copy after archiving (`EA/application/usecase/GetSignedCopyUseCase.kt:47-53`).
2. **Retention is 365 days, counted per file.** The signed copy counts from completion, the original from upload and the evidence certificate from its issue (`platform/contracts/.../retention/Retention.kt:34,59`). The live S3 rule is one 365-day expiration (`INFRA/docs/runbooks/documents-lifecycle-guard.md:9-12`).
3. **Nothing goes to Glacier in production yet.** "No Glacier transition is live" until #214 P5 (`INFRA/docs/runbooks/documents-lifecycle-guard.md:14`; `lv-retention-restore-iam.md:184-185`). The CFN `TransitionToGlacier` 90-day rule (`aws/bb-sign-aws.yml:291-295`) is not applied. The fact `RETENTION_IMPLEMENTED.documentsGlacierDays` is therefore wrong as a description of production. Restore on request ships in v0.34.0, but in production today a restore answers 200 (`AVAILABLE`). Archive dates are hidden (`app.retention.archive-dates-visible` default `false`, `application.yml:261`, not set in `INFRA/apps/bb-sign/edge-api.yaml`). Pages should describe the restore flow as conditional ("if your copy is archived") and never as something that happens at day 90 today.
4. **Evidence certificates are live.** Envelopes completed before the rollout instant show "Completado antes de habilitar los certificados de evidencia" (`INFRA/docs/runbooks/lv-legal-validity-release.md:80-81`; `FE/i18n/locales/es/envelopes.json:93`).
5. **Agreements are not part of the signer journey.** Pinning is off in production (`AGREEMENTS_PIN_AT_SEND "false"`, `INFRA/apps/bb-sign/edge-api.yaml:64`) and the platform ships no texts (`:68`). The certificate prints "Sin acuerdos adjuntos". No page in this scope may describe agreement acceptance by signers.
