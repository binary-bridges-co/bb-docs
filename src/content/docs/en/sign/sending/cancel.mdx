Cancelling closes an envelope before it completes: the signing links stop working and the envelope
becomes "Cancelled" for good. It is the only way to withdraw an envelope sent by mistake, with the
wrong document or with a signer missing.

## Who can cancel

Only someone who holds the cancel permission in the envelope's workspace:

- the **manager** of the workspace, and
- any **administrator of the organization**, who has access to every workspace.

A **contributor** can create and send envelopes, but not cancel them: on the detail page the
"Cancel Envelope" button does not appear. If you need to cancel and do not see the button, ask the
workspace manager or an administrator to do it. See
[Organizations, workspaces and roles](/en/sign/concepts/organizations-workspaces-roles/).

## From which statuses

An envelope can be cancelled in "Draft", "Sent" or "In Progress". A "Completed", "Cancelled" or
"Expired" envelope is already closed and does not offer the button.

Cancelling a "Draft" (an envelope that was created but not sent) keeps it from lingering in the
list; it affects nobody, because nobody received a link.

## How to cancel

<Steps>

1. Open the envelope from Envelopes.

2. At the bottom of the page, press "Cancel Envelope".

3. The "Cancel Envelope" dialog asks: "Are you sure you want to cancel this envelope? This action
   cannot be undone and all pending signatures will be invalidated." Confirm with "Cancel
   Envelope".

4. The status changes to "Cancelled" and the button disappears.

</Steps>

If the application answers "Could not cancel the envelope", reload the page: someone else may have
cancelled or completed it in the meantime. If the error persists, copy the reference the notice
shows and send it to your administrator.

## What happens to the signers

From the moment of cancellation, every signer's link opens the "Link Unavailable" page. Whoever had
already signed keeps their signature in the envelope's audit trail, but no signed PDF is generated,
because the envelope never completed.

<Aside type="caution" title="Nobody receives a cancellation email">
  Today bb-sign sends no email when you cancel. Signers who had not signed yet will still have the
  invitation in their inbox and, if enough time had passed, a reminder, and when they open the link
  they will only see that it is unavailable. Tell them yourself, especially if you are about to send
  them a new envelope: that way they know which one is the right one.
</Aside>

Automatic reminders stop with the cancellation. If your organization uses webhooks, the
`envelope.cancelled` event is published at that moment.

## It cannot be undone

A cancelled envelope is not reopened or resent. To move on, create a new envelope with the same
documents (they have to be uploaded again) and the same signers, who will receive new links.

The cancelled envelope stays visible in Envelopes, with its original documents and its audit trail,
until <Fact of={ENVELOPE.archiveAfterDays} unit="days" /> have passed since its creation; then it
moves to [Archived envelopes](/en/sign/sending/archived-envelopes/).
