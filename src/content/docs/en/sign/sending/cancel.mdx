Cancelling an envelope closes it before it completes: the signing links are deactivated
immediately and the envelope moves to the **Cancelled** status. Use this action to withdraw an
envelope sent by mistake, with the wrong document or with a signer missing, and then send a new one.

## Who can cancel

Envelopes can be cancelled by those who hold that permission in the envelope's workspace:

- the workspace **manager**;
- the organization's **administrators**, who have access to every workspace.

**Contributors** create and send envelopes; to cancel one, they ask the workspace manager or an
administrator. See
[Organizations, workspaces and roles](/en/sign/concepts/organizations-workspaces-roles/).

## Statuses that allow cancellation

You can cancel an envelope in **Draft**, **Sent** or **In Progress**. Envelopes in **Completed**,
**Cancelled** or **Expired** are already closed.

Cancelling a **Draft** removes it from the active list without affecting anyone, because no signer
has received a link.

## Cancel the envelope

<Steps>

1. Open the envelope from **Envelopes**.

2. At the bottom of the page, press **Cancel Envelope**.

3. The **Cancel Envelope** dialog asks for confirmation: "Are you sure you want to cancel this
   envelope? This action cannot be undone and all pending signatures will be invalidated." Confirm
   with **Cancel Envelope**.

4. The status changes to **Cancelled**.

</Steps>

If the application shows "Could not cancel the envelope", reload the page: someone else may have
cancelled or completed it at that moment. If the message repeats, copy the reference shown in the
notice and send it to your administrator.

## What happens to the signers

From the moment of cancellation, every signer's link opens the **Link Unavailable** page and
automatic reminders stop. Signatures already made remain in the envelope's audit trail. The signed
PDF is generated only for completed envelopes. If your organization uses webhooks, the
`envelope.cancelled` event is published at that moment.

<Aside type="caution" title="Let the signers know">
  Signers do not receive a cancellation notice. Tell them yourself, especially if you are about to
  send them a new envelope, so they know which one to sign.
</Aside>

## Send a replacement envelope

Cancellation is final. To continue, create a new envelope, upload the documents again and add the
signers, who will receive new links.

The cancelled envelope stays visible in **Envelopes**, with its original documents and its audit
trail, for <Fact of={ENVELOPE.archiveAfterDays} unit="days" /> from its creation; then it moves to
[Archived envelopes](/en/sign/sending/archived-envelopes/).
