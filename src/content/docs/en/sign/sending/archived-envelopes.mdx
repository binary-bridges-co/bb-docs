Closed envelopes do not stay in the Envelopes list forever. Some time after their creation, bb-sign
moves them to a separate view, "Archived Envelopes", where they remain visible but nothing can be
done with them any more. This page explains which ones, when and what changes.

## What is archived and when

Every night at <Fact of={ENVELOPE.archiveCron} />, a process looks for envelopes in the
"Completed", "Cancelled" or "Expired" status created more than
<Fact of={ENVELOPE.archiveAfterDays} unit="days" /> ago and moves them to the archive. Two details
matter:

- The period counts from the envelope's **creation**, not from its completion. An envelope that
  took long to complete is archived shortly after completing.
- An envelope in "Draft", "Sent" or "In Progress" is never archived, however old it is.
  [Cancel it](/en/sign/sending/cancel/) if it is not going to complete.

There is no way to archive an envelope by hand or to keep one from being archived.

## The view

The "Archived" entry in the menu opens "Archived Envelopes". A notice heads the list: "These
envelopes have been archived. They are read-only and data retrieval may take longer than usual."
Next to the title is the total ("N archived envelopes", with a plus sign when it exceeds the
counter cap) and the link "Back to active envelopes".

You can narrow by "Workspace", just as in Envelopes; the total narrows with the filter. Each row
shows the title, its labels, the number of signers, the creation and archive dates ("N signers ·
Created ... · Archived ..."), the workspace and the status it was archived with. Rows do not open:
there is no detail page for an archived envelope.

## Read-only

On an archived envelope you cannot:

- change the labels or cancel it (it was already closed);
- see its audit trail from the application;
- download the original or the signed PDF from the application.

Nothing is deleted when archiving: the envelope, its signers, its documents and its evidence are
kept according to [Scope and retention](/en/sign/legal/retention/). But from the application, what
remains is the row in the list. That is why it pays to download the signed PDFs as soon as the
envelope completes; see
[Download signed documents](/en/sign/sending/download-signed-documents/).

<Aside type="caution" title="The signer's download link stops working once archived">
  While the envelope is in Envelopes, each signer can return to their link and download their signed
  copy. As soon as the envelope is archived, that link opens "Link Unavailable" and there is no way
  to recover it from the application. If a signer needs their copy later, they will have to ask you
  for it; download and keep it while the envelope is still active.
</Aside>

## If you look for an envelope and cannot find it

If a completed, cancelled or expired envelope is no longer in Envelopes, look in Archived before
assuming it was deleted. If it is not there either, it may be in a workspace you have no access to:
ask an administrator to check.
