bb-sign keeps the Envelopes list focused on work in progress. After a while, closed envelopes move
to the **Archived Envelopes** view, where they remain available for read-only reference.

## Which envelopes are archived and when

Every night at <Fact of={ENVELOPE.archiveCron} />, bb-sign archives envelopes in the **Completed**,
**Cancelled** or **Expired** status created more than
<Fact of={ENVELOPE.archiveAfterDays} unit="days" /> ago.

- The period counts from the envelope's **creation**. An envelope that took long to complete is
  archived shortly after completing.
- Envelopes in **Draft**, **Sent** or **In Progress** stay in the active list. If one of them is not
  going to complete, [cancel it](/en/sign/sending/cancel/).

## Browse archived envelopes

The **Archived** menu entry opens **Archived Envelopes**. The view states: "These envelopes have
been archived. They are read-only and data retrieval may take longer than usual." Next to the title
are the total ("N archived envelopes") and the link **Back to active envelopes**.

You can filter by **Workspace**, just as in Envelopes; the total follows the filter. Each row
shows:

- the title and its labels;
- the number of signers and the creation and archive dates ("N signers · Created ... · Archived
  ...");
- the workspace and the status it was archived with.

## How the information is kept

Archiving an envelope deletes nothing: the envelope, its signers, its documents and its evidence
are kept according to [Scope and retention](/en/sign/legal/retention/). The archive view shows each
envelope's row; download the signed PDFs and review the audit trail while the envelope is in
**Envelopes**. See [Download signed documents](/en/sign/sending/download-signed-documents/).

<Aside type="caution" title="Give signers their copy before archiving">
  While the envelope is in **Envelopes**, each signer can return to their link and download their
  signed copy. Once archived, that link opens **Link Unavailable**. If a signer needs their copy
  later, send them the signed PDF you downloaded.
</Aside>

## Find a closed envelope

If a completed, cancelled or expired envelope no longer appears in **Envelopes**, look for it in
**Archived**. If it is not there either, it may belong to a workspace you do not have access to:
ask an administrator to check.
