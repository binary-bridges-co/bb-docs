bb-sign keeps the Envelopes list focused on work in progress. After a while, closed envelopes move
to the **Archived Envelopes** view, where they remain available read-only, with their documents, their
evidence and their audit trail.

## Which envelopes are archived and when

Every night at <Fact of={ENVELOPE.archiveCron} />, bb-sign archives envelopes in the **Completed**,
**Cancelled** or **Expired** status created more than
<Fact of={ENVELOPE.archiveAfterDays} unit="days" /> ago.

- The period counts from the envelope's **creation**. An envelope that took long to complete is
  archived shortly after completing.
- A completed envelope is only archived once its signed PDFs have been generated. If one is still
  missing <Fact of={ENVELOPE.stampHoldDays} unit="days" /> later, the envelope stays in **Envelopes**
  and the bb-sign team is alerted.
- Envelopes in **Draft**, **Sent** or **In Progress** stay in the active list. If one of them is not
  going to complete, [cancel it](/en/sign/sending/cancel/).

## Browse archived envelopes

The **Archived** menu entry opens **Archived Envelopes**. The view states: "These envelopes have
been archived. They are read-only and data retrieval may take longer than usual." Next to the title
are the total ("N archived envelopes") and the link **Back to active envelopes**.

You can filter by **Workspace**, just as in Envelopes; the total follows the filter. Each row
shows:

- the title and its labels;
- the number of signers and the creation and archive dates ("N signers · Created ... · Archived
  ...");
- the workspace and the status it was archived with.

## Open an archived envelope

Press a row's title to open the envelope. The page is the same as in **Envelopes**, with the notice
"Archived on \{date\}. This envelope is read-only; its documents are still available to download." From
it you can:

- download each document's **Original PDF** and **Signed PDF**, with the same permission as in
  **Envelopes**. See [Download signed documents](/en/sign/sending/download-signed-documents/);
- if the envelope completed, download its evidence certificate and the audit trail as JSON. See
  [Evidence certificate](/en/sign/concepts/evidence-certificate/);
- review the audit trail and the **Retention** section.

An archived envelope has no actions: its labels cannot be changed and it cannot be cancelled.

## Archived is not archive storage

Moving to **Archived** is a change of list, not of storage: the envelope's files download just as
before. Archive storage is something else: the terms allow a file more than
<Fact of={RETENTION_POLICY.archiveAfterDays} unit="days" /> old to be moved there, and it then has to
be restored before it can be downloaded. Today bb-sign does not move any file to that storage. See
[Retention and restore](/en/sign/sending/retention-and-restore/).

Archiving an envelope deletes nothing: the envelope, its signers, its documents and its evidence are
kept until each file's period ends, <Fact of={RETENTION_POLICY.retentionDays} unit="days" />. See
[Scope and retention](/en/sign/legal/retention/).

## Signers keep their access

Each signer's link still gives access to their signed copy after the
envelope moves to **Archived**, until their copy's retention period ends, counted from when the
envelope completed. If their copy were ever in archive storage, the signer can request it from their
own link. See [Sign and download](/en/sign/signing/sign-and-download/).

<Aside type="caution" title="Verifying an archived envelope">
  Today, scanning the QR code on the seal of an envelope that is already in **Archived** makes the
  verification page show **Invalid Signature**, even though the document is intact. A document signed
  since bb-sign <Fact of={DOCUMENTS.signedSha256Since} /> is confirmed with **Check a file**; an earlier
  one, with a PDF reader's signature panel. See [Verify a signed document](/en/sign/signing/verify-a-document/).
</Aside>

## Find a closed envelope

If a completed, cancelled or expired envelope no longer appears in **Envelopes**, look for it in
**Archived**. If it is not there either, it may belong to a workspace you do not have access to:
ask an administrator to check.
