In the **Signers** step of the **Create Envelope** wizard you set who signs. Together with the
**Signing order** chosen in **Details**, this step determines who receives the link at send and who
signs later, so each signature arrives at the right moment.

## Add the signers

The **Add Signers** screen shows one card per person with two fields:

| Field | Use |
|---|---|
| **Email** | Address that receives the signing link and, if you turned on OTP verification, the code. It identifies the signer throughout the process. |
| **Name** | Full name. It appears on the signing page ("Signing as ...") and in the seal on the document. |

**Add another signer** adds a card at the end of the list. With
<Fact of={SIGNING_ORDER.removeFromCards} /> cards or more, each one has a button with a trash icon
to remove it. Empty cards are discarded when you continue. If something is missing, the application
flags it with "Email is required" or "Name is required".

Each signer gets their own signing link. The same person can sign in several envelopes.

Review the list before you continue: the signers are fixed once the envelope is created. If you
need to change them later, [cancel the envelope](/en/sign/sending/cancel/) and create a new one.

## Sign in order

With **Signers sign in order** (sequential signing), the screen says: "Signers sign in this order. Drag a signer by the handle, or use Move up and Move down."

Each card shows its position as a number, 1 for the first. The signing order is the order of the
cards, top to bottom: there is no number to type. With
<Fact of={SIGNING_ORDER.reorderControlsFromCards} /> cards or more, each one also has:

| Control | Use |
|---|---|
| Drag handle (the dotted icon, left of the number) | Drag the card with the mouse or your finger to its new position. |
| **Move up** and **Move down** (the arrows, on the right) | Move the card one position. **Move up** is disabled on the first card and **Move down** on the last. |

The buttons show only an icon. A screen reader announces them with the signer's name, for example
"Move Ana Perez up" or "Remove Ana Perez"; a card without a name is called "Signer 2".

You can also reorder with the keyboard. Move the focus to the drag handle and press Space or Enter
to pick up the card, move it with the arrow keys, and press Space or Enter to drop it or Escape to
cancel. Every move is announced to screen readers, for example: "Ana Perez moved to position 1 of 3."

If drag and drop cannot load, the screen says "Drag and drop could not load. Use Move up and Move down."
and the **Move up** and **Move down** buttons keep working.

The positions define the turns:

- At send, only the signer in position 1 receives the email.
- When a signer signs, the one in the next position receives `It's your turn to sign: {title}`.
  Their link is generated at that moment, so the link expiry (by default
  <Fact of={ENVELOPE.linkTtlHoursDefault} unit="h" />) starts counting then.

If a signer opens their link before their turn, they see **Waiting for Previous Signers** and can
press **Check Again** later. The envelope page sums it up: "Sequential signing · Each signer is
emailed only once everyone before them has signed."

From the application, each turn has one signer. Several signers in the same turn can only be set
through the API, with the same `signOrder`; see
[From credential to signed document](/en/sign/developers/envelope-walkthrough/).

## Sign at once

With **Everyone signs at once** (parallel signing), the screen says: "Everyone is invited at once and can sign in any order."
The cards show no positions and no reorder controls. At send, every signer receives the email
`You have a document to sign: {title}` at the same time, and each signs whenever it suits them. The
envelope moves to **In Progress** with the first signature and to **Completed** with the last.

The envelope page shows: "Parallel signing · Everyone was emailed at once and can sign in any order."

If you change the **Signing order** in **Details**, the list keeps the order of the cards.

## Choose the right mode

| | At once | In order |
|---|---|---|
| Cards | No position | Position, drag handle, **Move up** and **Move down** |
| Who gets the link at send | Everyone | The signer in position 1 |
| Email to the next signers | Not applicable | `It's your turn to sign: {title}` when the previous one signs |
| While a signer has not signed | The others continue | The next ones wait |

Choose **in order** when one signature must come before the next, for example when the approver
signs after the requester. Otherwise, **at once** is faster and no signer waits for another.

## Review the list and follow each signer

The **Review** step shows the same list under **Signers (N)**, in the same order and without
controls. With signing in order, each signer carries their position; with signing at once, they do
not.

The envelope page shows the list the same way, with each signer's status: **Pending** (no link
yet), **Notified** (received the email), **Viewed** (opened the link) and **Signed**. If the API set
several signers in the same turn, they share the position and the next ones follow without gaps,
for example 1, 1, 2. If a signer is not going to sign, cancel the envelope or let it expire. See
[Send and track](/en/sign/sending/send-and-track/).
