In the **Signers** step of the **Create Envelope** wizard you set who signs. Together with the
**Signing order** chosen in **Details**, this step determines who receives the link at send and who
signs later, so each signature arrives at the right moment.

## Add the signers

The **Add Signers** screen shows one row per person with two fields:

| Field | Use |
|---|---|
| **Email** | Address that receives the signing link and, if you turned on OTP verification, the code. It identifies the signer throughout the process. |
| **Name** | Full name. It appears on the signing page ("Signing as ...") and in the seal on the document. |

**Add another signer** adds a row and the **Remove signer** icon deletes it. Empty rows are
discarded when you continue. If something is missing, the application flags it with "Email is
required" or "Name is required".

Each row generates its own signing link. The same person can sign in several envelopes.

Review the list before you continue: the signers are fixed once the envelope is created. If you
need to change them later, [cancel the envelope](/en/sign/sending/cancel/) and create a new one.

## Sign at once

With **Everyone signs at once** (parallel signing), the **Order** field is not shown. At send,
every signer receives the email `You have a document to sign: {title}` at the same time, and each
signs whenever it suits them. The envelope moves to **In Progress** with the first signature and to
**Completed** with the last.

The envelope page shows: "Parallel signing · Everyone was emailed at once. The order numbers below
are not enforced." In this mode, the numbers only reflect the order in which the rows were entered.

## Sign in order

With **Signers sign in order** (sequential signing), each row has an **Order** field with a number.
The numbers define turns:

- At send, the signers with the lowest number receive the email.
- When everyone in a turn has signed, those with the next number receive
  `It's your turn to sign: {title}`. Their link is generated at that moment, so the link expiry (by
  default <Fact of={ENVELOPE.linkTtlHoursDefault} unit="h" />) starts counting then.
- Signers with the same number form one turn and sign at the same time.

If a signer opens their link before their turn, they see **Waiting for Previous Signers** and can
press **Check Again** later. The envelope page sums it up: "Sequential signing · Each signer is
emailed only once everyone before them has signed."

## Choose the right mode

| | At once | In order |
|---|---|---|
| **Order** field | Not shown | One number per signer |
| Who gets the link at send | Everyone | The first turn |
| Email to the following turns | Not applicable | `It's your turn to sign: {title}` when the previous turn finishes |
| While a signer has not signed | The others continue | The following turns wait |

Choose **in order** when one signature must come before the next, for example when the approver
signs after the requester. Otherwise, **at once** is faster and no signer waits for another.

## Follow each signer

From the envelope page you can see each signer's status: **Pending** (no link yet), **Notified**
(received the email), **Viewed** (opened the link) and **Signed**. If a signer is not going to
sign, cancel the envelope or let it expire. See [Send and track](/en/sign/sending/send-and-track/).
