Once sent, an envelope moves on its own: each signer receives a link, signs, and bb-sign records
every step. Your job is to follow it. This page explains what each status means, how to find an
envelope in the list, what its detail page shows and what bb-sign does on its own so signers do not
forget.

## Statuses

The envelope status appears as a badge in the list, on the Dashboard and on the detail page:

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| Draft | Created but not sent. Nobody has a link. |
| Sent | Sent; no signer has signed yet. |
| In Progress | At least one signer signed and someone is still missing. |
| Completed | Everyone signed. The signed PDFs are generated next. |
| Cancelled | A manager or an administrator cancelled it. The links do not work. |
| Expired | The "Expires at" date passed without completion. The links do not work. |

Each signer has a status of their own: "Pending" (no link yet, which happens with signing in
order), "Notified" (received the email), "Viewed" (opened the link) and "Signed". The full flow is
in [Envelopes and signers](/en/sign/concepts/envelopes-and-signers/).

## The Envelopes list

"Envelopes" shows the envelopes of the workspaces you have access to, newest first, ten per page.
Next to the title is the total ("N total envelopes"); when there are more than
<Fact of={ENVELOPE.listCountCap} />, the figure is shown with a plus sign, as
"<Fact of={ENVELOPE.listCountCap} />+ envelopes", and the counter stops being exact.

The filters combine:

| Filter | Control |
|---|---|
| Workspace | "Workspace" selector, "All workspaces" by default. It only appears if you have access to more than one workspace. |
| Status | "Filter by status" selector. In the English interface it shows the raw values: ALL, DRAFT, SENT, IN_PROGRESS, COMPLETED, CANCELLED, EXPIRED. |
| Title | "Search by title..." field. It searches as you type. |
| Dates | "From date" to "To date", on the creation date. |
| Labels | "Filter by label...": choose the key, type the value and add it. Several can be stacked. |

Each active filter appears as a chip you can remove; "Clear all" removes them all. If nothing
matches, you see "No envelopes match your filters."; if there are no envelopes at all, "No
envelopes yet." with the link "Create your first one". Each row shows the title, up to two labels,
the workspace, the number of signers, the creation date and the status.

## The detail page

When you open an envelope you see its title, its identifier, the workspace and your role in it, and
the labels (which you can change with "+ Add label" if you are a contributor or manager). Below, by
section:

- **Fields.** "Status", "OTP Required" (Yes or No), "Created" and, if you set it, "Expires"; once
  the date has passed, it carries the suffix "(Expired)".
- **"Documents (N)".** Each file with the buttons "Original PDF" and, on a completed envelope,
  "Signed PDF". While the signed one is being generated, you see "Signed PDF processing...".
  Without download permission, the name appears but not the buttons, with the explanation "You can
  see this document's name but not download it. Ask an admin for auditor access." See
  [Download signed documents](/en/sign/sending/download-signed-documents/).
- **"Signers (N)".** The mode ("Sequential signing" or "Parallel signing" with its explanation)
  and, per signer, name, email, "Order #N", the signing date if they signed, and their status.
- **"Audit Trail (N events)".** Each event with its type, who caused it (the signer's email or
  "System"), the date and time and its hash. Only for auditors and managers of the workspace; the
  others see "The audit trail is available to auditors and managers of this workspace." See
  [Evidence and audit](/en/sign/concepts/evidence-and-audit/).
- **Actions.** The "Cancel Envelope" button, only for those who can cancel and only in Draft, Sent
  or In Progress. See [Cancel an envelope](/en/sign/sending/cancel/).

If you open an envelope that does not exist or that you cannot see, the answer is the same:
"Envelope not found, or you don't have access to it." bb-sign does not tell the two cases apart on
purpose.

## The Dashboard

The Dashboard summarizes the envelopes you can see in four figures: "Total Envelopes", "Completed",
"In Progress" (which adds up the envelopes in Sent and in In Progress) and "Drafts". Below, "Recent
Envelopes" lists the last four with their status, and "+ New" opens the wizard.

## Automatic reminders

You do not have to chase signers. Every day at <Fact of={REMINDERS.dailyRunTime} />, bb-sign sends
`Reminder: please sign {title}` to every signer who received their invitation more than
<Fact of={REMINDERS.thresholdHours} unit="h" /> ago and has not signed yet, with at least
<Fact of={REMINDERS.cooldownHours} unit="h" /> between two reminders to the same person. Reminders
stop when they sign, or when the envelope completes, is cancelled or expires.

There is no way to send a reminder by hand or to resend the link. If a signer says nothing arrived,
ask them to check their spam folder and to search for the subjects above; the sender is always the
same. See [The invitation email](/en/sign/signing/receiving-the-email/).

<Aside type="note" title="bb-sign does not notify you when an envelope completes">
  Today there is no email to the sender when an envelope completes. To find out, check the
  Envelopes list or the Dashboard. If your organization integrates systems, an administrator can set
  up a webhook for the `envelope.completed` event; see [Webhooks](/en/sign/admin/webhooks/).
</Aside>
