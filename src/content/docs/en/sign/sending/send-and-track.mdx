Once sent, the envelope moves forward on its own: each signer receives a link and signs, while
bb-sign records every step and reminds those who have not signed yet. From the Envelopes list, the
detail page and the Dashboard, you always know where each envelope stands.

## Envelope statuses

The status appears in the list, on the Dashboard and on the detail page:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Created and waiting to be sent. No signer has a link. |
| **Sent** | Sent; no signer has signed yet. |
| **In Progress** | At least one signer has signed and others remain. |
| **Completed** | Everyone signed. The signed PDFs are generated next. |
| **Cancelled** | A manager or an administrator cancelled it. The links are closed. |
| **Expired** | The **Expires at** date arrived before completion. The links are closed. |

Each signer also has a status of their own: **Pending** (no link yet, when signing in order),
**Notified** (received the email), **Viewed** (opened the link) and **Signed**. The full cycle is
described in [Envelopes and signers](/en/sign/concepts/envelopes-and-signers/).

## Find an envelope in the list

**Envelopes** shows the envelopes of the workspaces you have access to, newest first, ten per page.
Next to the title is the total ("N total envelopes"); from <Fact of={ENVELOPE.listCountCap} />
envelopes on, the figure is shown as "<Fact of={ENVELOPE.listCountCap} />+ envelopes".

The filters can be combined:

| Filter | Control |
|---|---|
| Workspace | **Workspace** selector, with **All workspaces** by default. It appears when you have access to more than one workspace. |
| Status | **Filter by status** selector, with the values ALL, DRAFT, SENT, IN_PROGRESS, COMPLETED, CANCELLED, EXPIRED. |
| Title | **Search by title...** field, which searches as you type. |
| Dates | **From date** and **To date**, on the creation date. |
| Labels | **Filter by label...**: choose the key, type the value and add it. You can combine several. |

Each active filter appears as a chip you can remove on its own; **Clear all** removes them all.
Each row shows the title, up to two labels, the workspace, the number of signers, the creation date
and the status.

## Use the detail page

When you open an envelope you see its title, its identifier, the workspace, your role in it and the
labels, which contributors and managers can change with **+ Add label**. The page is organized in
sections:

| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| Envelope details | **Status**, **OTP Required** (Yes or No), **Created** and, if set, **Expires**, with the suffix **(Expired)** once the date has passed. |
| **Documents (N)** | Each file with **Original PDF** and, on a completed envelope, **Signed PDF**. While the signed PDF is being generated, **Signed PDF processing...** is shown. See [Download signed documents](/en/sign/sending/download-signed-documents/). |
| **Signers (N)** | The mode (**Sequential signing** or **Parallel signing**) and, per signer, name, email, **Order #N**, signing date and status. |
| **Audit Trail (N events)** | Each event with its type, who triggered it (the signer's email or **System**), the date and time and its hash. Available to the workspace's auditors and managers. See [Evidence and audit](/en/sign/concepts/evidence-and-audit/). |
| Actions | **Cancel Envelope**, for those who can cancel, on envelopes in Draft, Sent or In Progress. See [Cancel an envelope](/en/sign/sending/cancel/). |

If an envelope does not exist or you do not have access to it, the application shows "Envelope not
found, or you don't have access to it." For security, the answer is the same in both cases.

## See the summary on the Dashboard

The **Dashboard** summarizes the envelopes you can see in four figures: **Total Envelopes**,
**Completed**, **In Progress** (which groups envelopes in Sent and in In Progress) and **Drafts**.
Below, **Recent Envelopes** shows the last four with their status, and **+ New** opens the wizard.

## Automatic reminders

bb-sign reminds signers who have a signature pending. Every day at
<Fact of={REMINDERS.dailyRunTime} />, it sends `Reminder: please sign {title}` to every signer who
received their invitation more than <Fact of={REMINDERS.thresholdHours} unit="h" /> ago and has not
signed yet, with at least <Fact of={REMINDERS.cooldownHours} unit="h" /> between two reminders to
the same person. Reminders end when the signer signs or when the envelope completes, is cancelled
or expires.

If a signer cannot find the email, ask them to check their spam folder and to search for the
subjects shown on this page. See [The invitation email](/en/sign/signing/receiving-the-email/).

<Aside type="tip" title="Know when an envelope completes">
  Check the Envelopes list or the Dashboard to see completed envelopes. If your organization
  integrates other systems, an administrator can set up a webhook for the `envelope.completed`
  event; see [Webhooks](/en/sign/admin/webhooks/).
</Aside>
