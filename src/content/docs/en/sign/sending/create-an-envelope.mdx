The first step of the "Create Envelope" wizard, "Envelope Details", decides how the envelope will
behave for its whole life: where it is kept, who sees it, how each signer is identified and until
when it can be signed. This page explains every field in the order it appears. The full walkthrough
is in [Your first envelope](/en/sign/getting-started/first-envelope/).

## Workspace

The "Workspace" selector lists the workspaces where you can create envelopes. General is selected
by default; the application shows it as "General (everyone)". The help text sums it up: "Only
people with access to this workspace will see the envelope."

Choose the workspace first: it decides who will be able to view, download and cancel the envelope.
An envelope does not move to another workspace after it is created. See
[Organizations, workspaces and roles](/en/sign/concepts/organizations-workspaces-roles/).

## Title

"Title *" is required and is the name the envelope carries everywhere: in the Envelopes list, on
the detail page and in the subject of the emails to the signer, such as
`You have a document to sign: {title}`. Use a title the signer will recognize.

## Labels

The "Labels" block only appears if an administrator defined label keys for your organization.
Choose a key in "Add label...", type the value and press "Add". A value has at most
<Fact of={LABELS.valueMaxChars} unit="characters" />. Labels are used to filter the Envelopes list
and can be changed later from the envelope page, even once it is sent. See
[Labels](/en/sign/concepts/labels/).

## Message to signers

Optional text saved with the envelope. Today that text appears neither in the invitation email nor
on the signing page: the signer does not see it. If you need to give instructions, do it on your
own or inside the document itself.

## Require OTP verification for signers

Checked by default. With this box, every signer must prove they control their email before
signing: bb-sign sends them by <Fact of={OTP.channel} /> a code of
<Fact of={OTP.digits} unit="digits" /> that is valid for <Fact of={OTP.validitySeconds} unit="s" />,
allows <Fact of={OTP.maxAttempts} unit="attempts" /> and can be requested again after
<Fact of={OTP.resendCooldownSeconds} unit="s" />. Once verified, the signer has
<Fact of={OTP.verifiedValidityMinutes} unit="min" /> to submit their signature.

Without the box, the link in the email is the signer's only identification. Uncheck it only when
evidence of control of the email does not matter to you. There is no SMS verification and there are
no access codes.

## Signing order

Two options, each with its explanation on screen:

- "Everyone signs at once": "Everyone is emailed at once and can sign in any order."
- "Signers sign in order": "Signers are emailed one at a time, in the order you set on the signers
  step."

With the second, the Signers step shows the "Order" field. What each signer receives and when is in
[Signers and order](/en/sign/sending/signers-and-order/).

## Link expiry (hours)

How long each signer's signing link is valid, in whole hours. By default,
<Fact of={ENVELOPE.linkTtlHoursDefault} unit="h" />. The clock starts when the link is generated:
at send, for every signer in parallel mode; when their turn comes, in sequential mode.

An expired link opens the "Link Unavailable" page. Today a signing link cannot be resent or
regenerated: if a signer let it expire, [cancel the envelope](/en/sign/sending/cancel/) and create
a new one. Set a validity that matches how long your signers take.

## Expires at (optional)

A date and time limit for the whole envelope. The help text says: "Leave empty for no expiration."
If you set it, it must be in the future.

Every <Fact of={ENVELOPE.expirySweepMinutes} unit="min" /> a process checks the envelopes in
"Sent" or "In Progress" whose date has passed and marks them "Expired". Their signers receive the
email `Signing request expired: {title}` and their links stop working. An expired envelope does not
reopen.

<Aside type="note" title="Link expiry and envelope expiration are not the same thing">
  Link expiry affects each signer separately and does not change the envelope status. Expiration
  closes the whole envelope. Without an expiration date, an envelope whose links have expired stays
  "Sent" until you cancel it.
</Aside>
