When an envelope completes, bb-sign generates a signed PDF for every document: the original with
each signer's seal, signed with the organization's certificate and its certificate chain. Every
document is sealed and any later change is detected. bb-sign also issues an evidence certificate for
the envelope. Download them from the envelope page and keep them in your organization's systems.

## Download the documents

In the **Documents (N)** section of the envelope page, each document shows its fingerprint
("SHA-256 of the original:") and offers these downloads:

| Button | Contents | Available |
|---|---|---|
| **Original PDF** | The file as it was uploaded, without seals. | From the envelope's creation. |
| **Signed PDF** | The document with each signer's seal, signed with the certificate. | When the envelope is **Completed** and the file has been generated. |

The signed PDF is generated a few moments after the last signature; meanwhile, the page shows
**Signed PDF processing...**. Reload the page to see the button. If the message remains, tell your
administrator.

The seal's contents (name, email, date, the image of the signature, drawn, typed or uploaded, a hash and a verification QR
code) and the role of the certificate are described in
[Electronic signature and seal](/en/sign/concepts/signature-and-seal/). To check a signed PDF you
received another way, see [Verify a signed document](/en/sign/signing/verify-a-document/).

## Download the evidence certificate

On a completed envelope, the same section ends with the **Evidence certificate** row. Once the
certificate is issued, it shows its fingerprint ("SHA-256 of the certificate:") and two buttons:

| Button | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Evidence certificate (PDF)** | The sealed certificate: documents, signers, how each one was identified and the envelope's timeline. |
| **Audit trail (JSON)** | The envelope's events, stored with the certificate when it was issued. |

While it is being issued, the row shows "Evidence certificate processing...". An envelope completed
before certificates existed shows "Completed before evidence certificates were enabled." and has no
certificate. If the row shows "It could not be generated; the team was notified.", the bb-sign team
has already been alerted: check the envelope again later. The full contents are described in
[Evidence certificate](/en/sign/concepts/evidence-certificate/).

## How long the download link lasts

Each document button uses a temporary link, valid for
<Fact of={ENVELOPE.downloadUrlSeconds} unit="s" /> from the moment the page opens. The evidence
certificate buttons ask for a link when pressed, valid for
<Fact of={EVIDENCE.downloadLinkMinutes} unit="minutes" />. If a download does not start, reload the
envelope page and press the button again. To share a document, send the downloaded file.

## Who can download

Users with the **auditor**, **contributor** or **manager** role in the envelope's workspace can
download, as can the organization's administrators. The same permission gives access to the evidence
certificate and to requesting the restore of an archived file. Users with the **viewer** role see
each document's name along with the message "You can see this document's name but not download it.
Ask an admin for auditor access." and, instead of the certificate, "The evidence certificate is
available to those who can download this envelope's documents."

Signers download their signed copy and their own copy of the evidence certificate from their link,
when they finish signing and from the email `Signing complete: {title}`. Their copy of the certificate
masks email addresses and leaves out the IP address and the browser. See
[Sign and download](/en/sign/signing/sign-and-download/).

## Download and retention periods

Once <Fact of={ENVELOPE.archiveAfterDays} unit="days" /> have passed since its creation, a completed
envelope moves to [Archived envelopes](/en/sign/sending/archived-envelopes/). From there it opens
read-only and its files still download the same way.

bb-sign keeps each file for <Fact of={RETENTION_POLICY.retentionDays} unit="days" />, a fixed,
non-negotiable period counted per file: the original from upload, the signed PDF from the envelope's
completion and the certificate from its issue. The **Retention** section of the envelope page shows
the date from which its files are no longer available. After that, retention is your organization's
responsibility. See [Scope and retention](/en/sign/legal/retention/).

If a file is archived, the page shows **Request restore** instead of the download button. Today
bb-sign does not move any file to archive storage; when a copy is archived, the procedure is described
in [Retention and restore](/en/sign/sending/retention-and-restore/).

<Aside type="tip" title="Download the envelope when it completes">
  As soon as an envelope becomes **Completed**, download the **Signed PDF** of every document and its
  evidence certificate, and store them in your organization's document system. An administrator can
  automate this step with the `envelope.completed` and `envelope.evidence_issued` webhooks; see
  [Webhooks](/en/sign/admin/webhooks/) and section 4 of the
  [retention and restore guide](/en/sign/legal/retention-guide/).
</Aside>
