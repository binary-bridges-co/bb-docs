When an envelope completes, bb-sign generates a signed PDF for every document: the original with
each signer's seal and the signature of the organization's certificate. This page explains where to
download it, how long the link lasts, who cannot do it and until when it is available.

## Original PDF and signed PDF

In the "Documents (N)" section of the envelope page, each file has up to two buttons:

| Button | What it downloads | When it appears |
|---|---|---|
| "Original PDF" | The file as you uploaded it, without seals. | Always, from the moment the envelope exists. |
| "Signed PDF" | The document sealed by each signer and signed with the certificate. | When the envelope is "Completed" and the file has been generated. |

A few moments pass between the last signer signing and the file being ready; meanwhile the button
is replaced by "Signed PDF processing...". Reload the page. If the text stays there for a long time,
tell your administrator.

What the seal contains (name, email, date, the drawn or typed signature, a hash and a QR code to
verify it) and what the certificate adds is in
[Electronic signature and seal](/en/sign/concepts/signature-and-seal/). How to check a signed PDF
that reaches you another way is in [Verify a signed document](/en/sign/signing/verify-a-document/).

## Download links expire quickly

Each button points to a temporary link valid for <Fact of={ENVELOPE.downloadUrlSeconds} unit="s" />
from the moment the page opens. If you press it later, the download fails: reload the envelope page
and press again. Do not copy or forward the link; share the downloaded file.

## Who can download

Downloading requires the **auditor**, **contributor** or **manager** role in the envelope's
workspace, or being an administrator of the organization. With the **viewer** role you see the name
of each document but no button, and the explanation: "You can see this document's name but not
download it. Ask an admin for auditor access."

Signers do not come here: they download their copy from their own link, when they finish signing
and from the email `Signing complete: {title}`. See
[Sign and download](/en/sign/signing/sign-and-download/).

## Until when

Two different deadlines limit downloading from the application:

1. **The envelope archive.** Once <Fact of={ENVELOPE.archiveAfterDays} unit="days" /> have passed
   since creation, a completed envelope moves to
   [Archived envelopes](/en/sign/sending/archived-envelopes/). That view offers no downloads, and
   the signer's link stops working.
2. **Storage.** Today, in production, files move to cold storage
   <Fact of={RETENTION_IMPLEMENTED.documentsGlacierDays} unit="days" /> after upload. From then on
   they cannot be downloaded immediately. A signer who opens their link sees: "This document is no
   longer available for download. Please contact the administrator." Restore on request does not
   exist yet.

The service policy fixes retention at <Fact of={RETENTION_POLICY.retentionDays} unit="days" />,
non-negotiable, and after that conservation is your organization's responsibility. What the policy
says and what the infrastructure does today are kept apart in
[Scope and retention](/en/sign/legal/retention/).

<Aside type="caution" title="Download and keep the signed PDF when the envelope completes">
  bb-sign is not a document archive. As soon as an envelope becomes "Completed", download the
  "Signed PDF" of every document and store it in your organization's system. If you integrate
  systems, an administrator can automate it with the `envelope.completed` webhook; see
  [Webhooks](/en/sign/admin/webhooks/).
</Aside>
