bb-sign is Binary Bridges' electronic signature platform. With it, an organization sends documents
for signature, identifies every signer, receives the documents sealed with its certificate and keeps
complete evidence of the process. Sending and tracking envelopes is also available through an
integration API.

The process is straightforward: you upload one or more PDFs, gather them in an **envelope**, say who
must sign and send it. Each signer receives a link by email, reviews the documents, confirms their
identity and signs. When the envelope completes, bb-sign seals every document and records the
evidence.

The application is available at <a href={URLS.app.value}>{URLS.app.value}</a>.

## What bb-sign offers

- **Integrity.** Every signed document is sealed, and any later change is detected. bb-sign records
  the SHA-256 fingerprint of every document and binds each signature to those fingerprints.
- **Identification.** Each signer is identified by their email and, when the organization requires
  it, by a one-time verification code.
- **A record of what was signed.** Which documents the signer saw (by their SHA-256 fingerprint),
  when they opened them and how they were identified is recorded with date and time.
- **Inconsistency detection.** The audit chain lets you check that no event was inserted or removed
  afterwards.
- **Probative value.** The evidence of the process (who, what, when and from where) can be consulted
  on each envelope and is delivered as a PDF
  [evidence certificate](/en/sign/concepts/evidence-certificate/) that the organization and each
  signer can download.
- **Public verification.** Anyone who has the signed PDF can confirm, without an account, that bb-sign
  produced it. See [Verify a signed document](/en/sign/signing/verify-a-document/).
- **Traceability.** Every relevant action on an envelope is kept in its history.

bb-sign keeps signed documents and their evidence for a fixed period of
<Fact of={RETENTION_POLICY.retentionDays} unit="days" />, counted for each file; from then on, keeping
them is the organization's responsibility, as described in
[Scope and retention](/en/sign/legal/retention/). If a file were ever archived, it can be requested
again; see [Retention and restore](/en/sign/sending/retention-and-restore/).

For each document's seal to carry your organization's name and for PDF readers to recognize it as
trusted, we recommend configuring your [own certificate](/en/sign/admin/own-certificate/).
