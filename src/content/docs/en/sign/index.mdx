bb-sign is Binary Bridges' electronic signature platform. An organization uploads PDF documents,
gathers them in an **envelope**, says who must sign and sends it. Each signer receives a link by
email, reviews the documents, verifies their identity if the organization asked for it, and signs.
When the envelope completes, bb-sign seals every document with a digital certificate and keeps the
evidence of the whole process.

The application lives at <a href={URLS.app.value}>{URLS.app.value}</a>.

## What bb-sign does

bb-sign covers one part of the signing process, and covers it completely:

- **Integrity.** Every signed document is sealed; any later alteration is detectable.
- **Identification.** The signer is identified by their email and, when the organization requires
  it, by a one-time code.
- **Compliance with agreements.** What the signer saw and accepted is recorded with date and time.
- **Inconsistency detection.** The audit chain lets you check that nothing was inserted or deleted
  afterwards.
- **Probative value.** The evidence (who, what, when, from where) can be consulted and downloaded.
- **Traceability.** Every relevant action on an envelope is in its history.

## What bb-sign does not do

<Aside type="caution" title="Conservation is the customer's responsibility">
  bb-sign keeps signed documents and their evidence for a **fixed, non-negotiable** period of
  <Fact of={RETENTION_POLICY.retentionDays} unit="days" />. After that, keeping them is the
  responsibility of the organization that sent them. The details are in
  [Scope and retention](/en/sign/legal/retention/).
</Aside>

- bb-sign provides **electronic signature** (*firma electrónica*, Decreto 2364 de 2012). It is not
  *firma digital* in the terms of article 28 of Ley 527 de 1999. Each customer, through its legal
  office, decides whether the mechanism suits its documents. See
  [Electronic and digital signature](/en/sign/legal/electronic-vs-digital-signature/).
- The seal bb-sign places on each document uses, by default, a Binary Bridges **testing
  certificate**. For third parties to trust the seal, the organization must configure its own
  certificate. See [Your own certificate](/en/sign/admin/own-certificate/).
- There are no templates, no bulk sending and no drag-and-drop fields on the PDF. What exists is in
  this documentation; what is not here does not exist yet.
