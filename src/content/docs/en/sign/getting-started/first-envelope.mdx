An **envelope** gathers one or more PDF documents, the people who must sign them and the conditions
of the signature. With the **Create Envelope** wizard, opened from the **New Envelope** button in the
side menu, you prepare and send it in five steps: **Details**, **Documents**, **Signers**,
**Review** and **Done**.

As you move through it, the wizard keeps a draft in your browser ("Draft saved in this browser.
Nothing is created until you send."). If you close the tab and come back, you continue where you
left off. To discard the draft, use **Start over**.

## Before you start

- You need permission to create envelopes in at least one workspace. If you do not see the **New
  Envelope** button, ask an administrator for the Contributor or Manager role in a workspace. See
  [Organizations, workspaces and roles](/en/sign/concepts/organizations-workspaces-roles/).
- Have the PDFs to be signed at hand, up to <Fact of={DOCUMENTS.maxUploadMb} unit="MB" /> each.
- Have the email and full name of every signer.

## Create and send the envelope

<Steps>

1. **Details.** In **Envelope Details**, fill in the **Workspace** (General by default) and the
   **Title**, the only required fields. You can also add **Labels**, a **Message to signers**, the
   option **Require OTP verification for signers** (on by default), the **Signing order**
   (**Everyone signs at once** or **Signers sign in order**), the **Link expiry (hours)** (by
   default <Fact of={ENVELOPE.linkTtlHoursDefault} unit="h" />) and the date **Expires at
   (optional)**. Press **Next**. Every field is explained in
   [Create an envelope](/en/sign/sending/create-an-envelope/).

2. **Documents.** In **Upload Documents**, press **Click to upload PDF** and choose one or more PDF
   files. Each file uploads right away and appears in the list. See
   [Attach documents](/en/sign/sending/documents/).

3. **Signers.** In **Add Signers**, type the **Email** and **Name** of each person and use **Add
   another signer** to add more. If you chose signing in order, you also see the **Order** field.
   See [Signers and order](/en/sign/sending/signers-and-order/).

4. **Review.** In **Review & Send**, check the summary: title, workspace, OTP verification,
   sequential signing, documents, signers and labels. Press **Send Envelope** and confirm with
   **Send** in the **Send Envelope?** dialog.

5. **Done.** The **Envelope Sent!** screen shows each signer's signing link with a **Copy** button.
   bb-sign has already emailed each signer their link (with signing in order, the first ones in the
   order). From here you can go to **View Envelope Details** or **Create Another**.

</Steps>

## What happens when you send

On send, the envelope moves from **Draft** to **Sent** and every signer receives a unique link by
email. From that moment the documents and the list of signers are fixed, which guarantees that
everyone signs exactly the same thing. If you need to correct something,
[cancel the envelope](/en/sign/sending/cancel/) and create a new one.

From then on, follow its progress from **Envelopes**. See
[Send and track](/en/sign/sending/send-and-track/).

## Finish a pending send

**Send Envelope** creates the envelope with everything you gathered and then sends it. If the
envelope is created but the send does not complete (for example, because your organization reached
a limit of its plan), the envelope stays in **Draft** and the wizard says so:

> The envelope was created but not sent. Sending again sends it as it is; to change it, open it
> from Envelopes.

Once the cause is resolved, press **Send Envelope** again. If you close the wizard and come back
later, it recognizes the envelope ("You already created this envelope, but it was not sent.") and
offers **Send the envelope you already created**.

From **Envelopes** you can also open that draft to change its labels or cancel it. If you need
different documents or signers, cancel it and create a new one.

## What the signer sees

The signer does not need an account. They receive an email in English with the subject
`You have a document to sign: {title}`, open the link, review every document, enter the
verification code if you requested one, draw or type their signature and download their copy at the
end. Knowing that path helps you answer their questions.
