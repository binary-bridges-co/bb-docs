An **envelope** gathers one or more PDF documents, the people who must sign them and the conditions
of the signature. You create it with the "Create Envelope" wizard, reached through the "New
Envelope" button in the side menu. The wizard has five steps: Details, Documents, Signers, Review
and Done.

While you move through it, nothing exists on the server yet: the wizard keeps a draft in your
browser and says so ("Draft saved in this browser. Nothing is created until you send."). If you
close the tab and come back, you pick up where you left off; "Start over" discards that draft.

## Before you start

- You need to be able to create envelopes in at least one workspace. If you do not see the "New
  Envelope" button, ask an administrator for the contributor or manager role in a workspace. See
  [Organizations, workspaces and roles](/en/sign/concepts/organizations-workspaces-roles/).
- Have the PDFs to be signed at hand, each up to <Fact of={DOCUMENTS.maxUploadMb} unit="MB" />.
- Have the email and full name of every signer.

## The five steps

<Steps>

1. **Details.** The "Envelope Details" screen asks for the "Workspace" (General by default), the
   "Title *", the "Labels" if your organization uses them, a "Message to signers", the checkbox
   "Require OTP verification for signers" (checked by default), the "Signing order" ("Everyone
   signs at once" or "Signers sign in order"), the "Link expiry (hours)" (by default
   <Fact of={ENVELOPE.linkTtlHoursDefault} unit="h" />) and the date "Expires at (optional)". Only
   the title and the workspace are required. Press "Next". Every field is explained in
   [Create an envelope](/en/sign/sending/create-an-envelope/).

2. **Documents.** In "Upload Documents", press "Click to upload PDF" and choose one or more files.
   Each file uploads right away and appears in the list. PDF only. Until there is at least one, the
   wizard warns: "Upload at least one document to continue." See
   [Attach documents](/en/sign/sending/documents/).

3. **Signers.** In "Add Signers", type the "Email" and "Name" of each person and use "Add another
   signer" to add rows. The "Order" field only appears if you chose signing in order. See
   [Signers and order](/en/sign/sending/signers-and-order/).

4. **Review.** The "Review & Send" screen summarizes "Title:", "Workspace:", "OTP Required:",
   "Sequential Signing:", "Documents:", "Signers:" and "Labels:". Below it reminds you: "Sending
   will generate unique signing links for each signer. This cannot be undone." Press "Send
   Envelope". A dialog asks "Send Envelope?" and explains: "This will generate unique signing links
   for each signer and send the envelope. This action cannot be undone." Confirm with "Send".

5. **Done.** The "Envelope Sent!" screen shows each signer's signing link with a "Copy" button.
   bb-sign has already emailed each of them that same link (if you chose signing in order, only
   the first ones). From here you can go to "View Envelope Details" or "Create Another".

</Steps>

## What happens when you send

Sending is the point of no return. The envelope moves from "Draft" to "Sent", every signer receives
a unique link by email, and the documents and the list of signers are frozen: they can no longer be
changed, neither from the application nor through the API. If you made a mistake, the only way out
is to [cancel the envelope](/en/sign/sending/cancel/) and create another.

After sending, you follow progress from Envelopes. See [Send and track](/en/sign/sending/send-and-track/).

## If the envelope was created but not sent

Under the hood, "Send Envelope" does two things: first it creates the envelope with everything you
gathered, then it sends it. If the first succeeds and the second fails (for example, because your
organization reached a limit of its plan), the envelope exists in the "Draft" status and the wizard
tells you:

> The envelope was created but not sent. Sending again sends it as it is; to change it, open it
> from Envelopes.

From then on the previous steps are locked. You can press "Send Envelope" again once the cause of
the failure is resolved. If you close and come back later, the wizard recognizes it ("You already
created this envelope, but it was not sent.") and offers "Send the envelope you already created".

From Envelopes, a draft created this way can be opened to change its labels or to cancel it. Its
documents and signers cannot be changed from the application: if you need different ones, cancel it
and create a new one.

## What the signer sees

The signer has no account. They receive an email in English with the subject
`You have a document to sign: {title}`, open the link, review every document, enter the
verification code if you asked for one, draw or type their signature and download their copy at the
end. Knowing that path helps you answer their questions:
