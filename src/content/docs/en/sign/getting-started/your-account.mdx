Nobody signs up for bb-sign on their own. An administrator of your organization creates your user
and you receive an invitation by email. With it you choose a password and enter
<a href={URLS.app.value}>{URLS.app.value}</a>. This page covers that first day: the invitation, the
login and what you will see once inside.

## The invitation

When the administrator creates you, the application shows them the notice "User will receive an
email to set their password." That email comes from <code>{URLS.identitySenderAddress.value}</code>
and contains a link to define your password. The link is valid for
<Fact of={AUTH.invitationLinkHours} unit="h" /> from the moment it is sent.

If the link has expired, the page tells you it is no longer valid. Ask the administrator to use
"Resend invitation" on the Users page: you receive a new email with a new link, and the clock
starts again.

If the email never arrives, check your spam folder and confirm with the administrator that your
address is spelled correctly. They can resend the invitation as many times as needed.

## First login

<Steps>

1. Open the link in the invitation.

2. Type a password and confirm it. bb-sign asks for nothing else.

3. Go to <a href={URLS.app.value}>{URLS.app.value}</a> and sign in with your email and the password
   you just set. You land on the Dashboard.

</Steps>

From then on you sign in from that address. If you forget the password, the login screen offers to
reset it; the reset email comes from the same address as the invitation. To leave, use "Logout" at
the bottom of the side menu.

## What you see depending on your role

Your role shows under your name at the bottom of the side menu: "Org Admin" or "Org Member". The
menu changes with that role.

| Menu entry | Member | Administrator |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard | Yes | Yes |
| Envelopes | Yes | Yes |
| Archived | Yes | Yes |
| New Envelope | Only if you can create envelopes in some workspace | Yes |
| Labels | No | Yes |
| Users | No | Yes |
| Storage | No | Yes |
| Settings | No | Yes |

A member does not see Labels, Users, Storage or Settings. If you open one of those addresses by
hand, the application answers "Access Denied" and says your role does not have permission to view
that page. It is not an error: it is the limit of the role.

What you can do with each envelope does not depend on the organization role but on your role in the
**workspace** where the envelope lives: view, download, create, send or cancel. It is explained in
[Organizations, workspaces and roles](/en/sign/concepts/organizations-workspaces-roles/).

## Language

The application is available in Spanish and English. The "Language" selector sits in the top right
corner of every page, with the options "Español" and "English". The choice is saved in your
browser. If you never choose, bb-sign uses the browser language and, if it is neither of the two,
English.

<Aside type="note" title="Emails to signers are in English">
  The application language is for you only. The emails bb-sign sends to signers (invitation,
  verification code, reminder, envelope completed) are always in English, whatever your language or
  the signer's. If your signers do not read English, tell them yourself that the email is coming
  and what to do with it.
</Aside>

## Theme

Next to the language there is a button to switch between light and dark mode: "Switch to dark
mode" or "Switch to light mode", depending on which is active. It is saved in your browser too.
