Your bb-sign account is created by an administrator of your organization. You receive an invitation
by email, set your password and start working at <a href={URLS.app.value}>{URLS.app.value}</a>.
This page explains how to activate the account, what you will find when you sign in and how to
adjust the application to your preference.

## Receive the invitation

When the administrator creates your user, bb-sign sends you an email from
<code>{URLS.identitySenderAddress.value}</code> with a link to set your password. The link is valid
for <Fact of={AUTH.invitationLinkHours} unit="h" /> from the moment it is sent.

If the link is no longer valid, ask the administrator to use **Resend invitation** on the **Users**
page. You will receive a new email with a new link, and the period starts again.

If the email is not in your inbox, check your spam folder and confirm with the administrator that
your address is spelled correctly. The invitation can be resent as many times as needed.

## Sign in for the first time

<Steps>

1. Open the link in the invitation.

2. Type a password and confirm it.

3. Sign in at <a href={URLS.app.value}>{URLS.app.value}</a> with your email and the password you
   just set. The application takes you to the **Dashboard**.

</Steps>

From then on, sign in at that same address. If you forget your password, use the reset option on
the sign-in screen; the email comes from the same address as the invitation. To leave, use
**Logout** at the bottom of the side menu.

## What you see for your role

Your role appears under your name at the bottom of the side menu: **Org Admin** or **Org Member**.
The menu adapts to that role.

| Menu entry | Member | Administrator |
|---|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Yes | Yes |
| **Envelopes** | Yes | Yes |
| **Archived** | Yes | Yes |
| **New Envelope** | If you can create envelopes in some workspace | Yes |
| **Labels** | No | Yes |
| **Users** | No | Yes |
| **Storage** | No | Yes |
| **Settings** | No | Yes |

The administration pages are reserved for administrators. If a member opens one of them directly,
the application shows "Access Denied".

What you can do with each envelope (view, download, create, send or cancel) depends on your role in
the **workspace** where the envelope lives. You will find the details in
[Organizations, workspaces and roles](/en/sign/concepts/organizations-workspaces-roles/).

## Choose the language

The application is available in Spanish and English. The **Language** selector is in the top right
corner of every page, with the options **Español** and **English**, and your choice is saved in the
browser. If you do not choose one, bb-sign uses the browser language and, if it is neither of the
two, English.

<Aside type="note" title="Emails to signers are sent in English">
  The language you choose applies to the application. The emails bb-sign sends to signers
  (invitation, verification code, reminder and envelope completed) are sent in English. If your
  signers do not read English, let them know in advance that they will receive that email and what
  to do with it.
</Aside>

## Change the theme

Next to the language selector there is a button to switch between light and dark mode: **Switch to
dark mode** or **Switch to light mode**, depending on which is active. Your choice is also saved in
the browser.
