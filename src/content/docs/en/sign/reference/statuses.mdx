This page is the reference for every status the application and the API show, with the internal name
(the one that travels in the API and in webhooks) and the label you see on screen in English. The
concepts behind envelope and signer statuses are in
[Envelopes and signers](/en/sign/concepts/envelopes-and-signers/).

## Envelope statuses

<StatusFlow locale="en" of="envelope" />

An envelope becomes `EXPIRED` only if an expiry date was set when it was created. Cancelling is the
only transition a person starts after sending; expiring and completing are produced by the flow
itself. There is no "declined" or "voided" status: a signer cannot decline today.

## Signer statuses

<StatusFlow locale="en" of="signer" />

In an envelope with sequential signing, signers in later turns stay `PENDING` until the previous turn
finishes; then they receive their link and become `NOTIFIED`.

## Webhook deliveries

Every event a webhook is subscribed to produces a delivery, and every delivery may have several
attempts. The **Settings** page, **Webhooks** tab, shows the history with these labels.

Retries follow the ladder {WEBHOOKS.retryLadder.value.join(', ')} with a random variation of
<Fact of={WEBHOOKS.retryJitterPct} unit="%" />. After <Fact of={WEBHOOKS.disableAfterLadders} />
consecutive exhausted ladders, bb-sign disables the endpoint and emails the administrators. See
[Webhooks](/en/sign/admin/webhooks/).

### Outcome of each attempt

| Outcome | Label in the application | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| `SUCCESS` | Accepted | Your endpoint answered 2xx. |
| `HTTP_ERROR` | Rejected | Your endpoint answered, but with a code other than 2xx. Check your logs with the delivery's `X-BBSign-Event-Id`. |
| `TRANSPORT_ERROR` | Unreachable | No answer at all: DNS, connection refused, invalid TLS or timeout. Check that the URL is public and HTTPS. |

## API credentials

Credentials are listed in **Settings**, **API credentials** tab.

| Status | Label in the application | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Active | Active | The credential can obtain tokens and call the API. |
| Revoked, draining | Revoked — draining | You just revoked it. It cannot obtain new tokens, but the tokens it already issued keep working until they expire, at most <Fact of={AUTH.tokenLifetimeSeconds} unit="seconds" /> after they were issued. The row shows "Tokens this credential already issued stop working at" with the time. |
| Revoked | Revoked | No token from this credential works. It stays listed for <Fact of={AUTH.revokedCredentialListedDays} unit="days" /> and then disappears. |

Rotating a credential does not change its status: the previous secret stops working immediately and
the new one is shown once. See [API credentials](/en/sign/admin/api-credentials/).
