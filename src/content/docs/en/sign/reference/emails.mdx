bb-sign keeps signers, administrators and users informed with automatic emails at every key moment
of the process. This page lists all of them. Subjects are shown exactly as they are sent; `{title}` is
the envelope title.

## Senders

Envelope emails (invitations, codes, reminders, envelope completed, envelope expired and the webhook
notice) and the certificate expiry and restore notices are sent from
**<Fact of={URLS.senderAddress} />**. Account emails (invitation to the application, password reset
and email verification) are sent from **<Fact of={URLS.identitySenderAddress} />**. If your
organization filters incoming mail, ask your IT team to allow both addresses.

Reminders are sent once a day to signers whose invitation is older than
<Fact of={REMINDERS.thresholdHours} unit="hours" />, with at most one every
<Fact of={REMINDERS.cooldownHours} unit="hours" /> per signer. In the expiry notice's subject, N is the
number of days left. The subjects of account emails are set by the identity service, which is why
they appear in parentheses.

The envelope completed email waits for the evidence certificate for at most
<Fact of={EVIDENCE.degradedAfterHours} unit="hours" />, but does not mention it: it only links to the
signer's signed copy and says when the signed documents are still being prepared. See [Evidence certificate](/en/sign/concepts/evidence-certificate/).

The restore notice is only sent when the restore of an archived file ends, and today no file is moved
to archive storage. API credentials do not receive this email: their integrations learn of it through the
`document.restored` webhook.{RESTORE.emailLinksToEnvelopeOrSigningPage.value && ' The email attaches no files: it links to the envelope page or, when a signer asked, to their signing page.'}{RESTORE.emailGenericWithoutConfirmedAccess.value && ' If bb-sign cannot confirm that the user can still download the envelope\'s documents, the email is generic: no envelope title and no file names.'} See [Retention and restore](/en/sign/sending/retention-and-restore/).

<Aside type="note" title="Language of envelope emails">
  The envelope emails signers receive are sent in English. If your signers do not read English, let
  them know through your own channels which email they will receive and from which address. See
  [The invitation email](/en/sign/signing/receiving-the-email/).
</Aside>

## Tracking from the application

These moments are handled in the application or through webhooks:

- **Envelope completed.** Whoever sent the envelope sees the completion in the application or
  receives it by webhook with the `envelope.completed` event.
- **Envelope cancelled.** When you cancel an envelope, the link of pending signers stops being
  available. Let those signers know yourself. See [Cancel an envelope](/en/sign/sending/cancel/).
- **Certificate renewal.** The expiry notice goes to administrators; renew the certificate ahead of
  time and upload it before it expires. See [Your own certificate](/en/sign/admin/own-certificate/).
