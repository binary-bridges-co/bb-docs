bb-sign keeps signers, administrators and users informed with automatic emails at every key moment
of the process. This page lists all of them. Subjects are shown exactly as they are sent; `{title}` is
the envelope title.

## Senders

Envelope emails (invitations, codes, reminders, envelope completed, envelope expired and the webhook
notice) are sent from **<Fact of={URLS.senderAddress} />**. Account emails (invitation to the
application, password reset and email verification) are sent from
**<Fact of={URLS.identitySenderAddress} />**. If your organization filters incoming mail, ask your IT
team to allow both addresses.

Reminders are sent once a day to signers whose invitation is older than
<Fact of={REMINDERS.thresholdHours} unit="hours" />, with at most one every
<Fact of={REMINDERS.cooldownHours} unit="hours" /> per signer. The subjects of account emails are set
by the identity service, which is why they appear in parentheses.

<Aside type="note" title="Language of envelope emails">
  The emails signers receive are sent in English. If your signers do not read English, let them know
  through your own channels which email they will receive and from which address. See
  [The invitation email](/en/sign/signing/receiving-the-email/).
</Aside>

## Tracking from the application

These moments are handled in the application or through webhooks:

- **Envelope completed.** Whoever sent the envelope sees the completion in the application or
  receives it by webhook with the `envelope.completed` event.
- **Envelope cancelled.** When you cancel an envelope, the link of pending signers stops being
  available. Let those signers know yourself. See [Cancel an envelope](/en/sign/sending/cancel/).
- **Certificate expiry.** Keep track of your organization's certificate expiry date and renew it
  ahead of time. See [Your own certificate](/en/sign/admin/own-certificate/).
