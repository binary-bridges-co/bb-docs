This is the complete list of the emails bb-sign sends today. If an email is not here, bb-sign does
not send it. Subjects are shown as they go out; `{title}` is the envelope title.

## Senders

Envelope emails (invitations, codes, reminders, completed, expired and the webhook notice) come from
**<Fact of={URLS.senderAddress} />**. Identity emails (invitation to the application, password reset,
email verification) come from **<Fact of={URLS.identitySenderAddress} />**. Ask your IT team to allow
both addresses if your organization filters incoming mail.

Reminders go out once a day, to signers whose invitation is older than
<Fact of={REMINDERS.thresholdHours} unit="hours" />, and never more than one every
<Fact of={REMINDERS.cooldownHours} unit="hours" /> to the same signer. The subjects of the identity
emails are defined by the identity server's template, not by a bb-sign template; that is why they
appear in parentheses.

<Aside type="note" title="Envelope emails are English only">
  The application is available in Spanish and English, but the emails signers receive go out in
  English only today. If your signers do not read English, tell them through your own channel what
  they will receive and from which address. See
  [The invitation email](/en/sign/signing/receiving-the-email/).
</Aside>

## What bb-sign does not send

- **No email to the sender when the envelope completes.** Whoever sent the envelope sees it in the
  application or receives it by webhook (`envelope.completed`).
- **No cancellation email.** When an envelope is cancelled, signers who had not signed yet simply find
  the link unavailable. See [Cancel an envelope](/en/sign/sending/cancel/).
- **No expiry notice email** for the organization's certificate.
- **No SMS.** The one-time code always arrives by email.
