Each term appears with its English name as used in the API, its Spanish name (the one the law and the
Spanish application use) and, where it applies, the label the application shows. The links lead to
the page that explains it in detail.

## The model

- **Organization (organización).** The bb-sign customer. It groups users, workspaces, envelopes, the
  seal certificate, API credentials and webhooks. Each organization's data is isolated from every
  other organization's. See
  [Organizations, workspaces and roles](/en/sign/concepts/organizations-workspaces-roles/).
- **Workspace (espacio de trabajo).** A division of the organization that holds envelopes. Each
  person or credential has a role in each workspace. In the application: "Workspace".
- **General.** The workspace every organization has from the moment it is created. Every member of
  the organization takes part in it as a contributor; that role is fixed.
- **Role (rol).** The set of actions someone can perform. There are organization roles
  (administrator, member, API credential) and workspace roles (viewer, auditor, contributor,
  manager). See [Permissions matrix](/en/sign/admin/permissions/).
- **Envelope (sobre).** The unit of work: one or more PDF documents, one or more signers and the
  sending options (order, one-time code, link validity, expiry). See
  [Envelopes and signers](/en/sign/concepts/envelopes-and-signers/).
- **Signer (firmante).** The person who must sign an envelope. Identified by their email; no bb-sign
  account needed.
- **Sign order (orden de firma).** Each signer's turn when the envelope uses sequential signing.
  Signers in the same turn sign in parallel.
- **Label (etiqueta).** A key and value pair the organization defines and assigns to its envelopes to
  filter them. See [Labels](/en/sign/concepts/labels/).
- **Archived (archivados).** The view that completed, cancelled or expired envelopes move to once a
  set period has passed since their creation. It organizes the envelope list and is independent of
  the retention policy: an archived envelope is read-only and its documents remain available to
  download, from the signer's link too. It is not the same as file archiving (see below). See
  [Archived envelopes](/en/sign/sending/archived-envelopes/).

## Signature, seal and evidence

- **Firma electrónica (electronic signature).** bb-sign's signature mechanism: a reliable method that
  identifies the signer and records their approval. See
  [How bb-sign signatures are valid](/en/sign/legal/electronic-vs-digital-signature/).
- **Firma digital (digital signature).** A legal figure distinct from electronic signature, based on a
  personal certificate issued to the signer by an ECD. See
  [How bb-sign signatures are valid](/en/sign/legal/electronic-vs-digital-signature/).
- **Signature (firma).** In bb-sign, the mark the signer draws or types, stamped into the document
  with their name, email and the date. See
  [Electronic signature and seal](/en/sign/concepts/signature-and-seal/).
- **Seal (sello).** The cryptographic signature bb-sign applies to each document when the envelope
  completes, with the organization's certificate. It guarantees the document's integrity and shows
  which organization sealed it; the signer's identity is established by the evidence. See
  [Your own certificate](/en/sign/admin/own-certificate/).
- **One-time code (OTP, código de un solo uso).** The numeric code the signer receives by email and
  types before signing, when the organization requires it on the envelope. See
  [Review and verify identity](/en/sign/signing/review-and-identity/).
- **Audit trail (cadena de auditoría).** The hash-chained record of everything that happened to an
  envelope: who, what, when and from where. Every envelope, every organization and the platform have
  their own chain; events are only ever added and bb-sign verifies the chains every night. In the
  application: "Audit Trail". See [Evidence and audit](/en/sign/concepts/evidence-and-audit/).
- **Actor (actor).** Whoever performed an event of the audit trail: a user, an API credential (with
  the **API** badge), a signer or **System**. Older events that did not keep it show "Actor not
  recorded".
- **Identification method (método de identificación).** How a signer was identified: `EMAIL_LINK`
  (the link sent by email) or `EMAIL_LINK_OTP` (the link and a one-time code). It is recorded with
  every signature.
- **SHA-256 fingerprint (huella SHA-256).** A digest of a file's content that changes completely if a
  single byte changes. bb-sign records the original's on upload ("SHA-256 of the original:") and that
  of every signed document. See [Documents](/en/sign/concepts/documents/).
- **Document set fingerprint (huella del conjunto de documentos).** The fingerprint that combines the
  fingerprints of every document in an envelope. Each signature is bound to it.
- **Seal version (versión del sello).** The way the seal's hash is computed. Version 2 includes the
  document set fingerprint and the fingerprint of the certificate that sealed; version 1 is that of
  earlier seals. See [Evidence and audit](/en/sign/concepts/evidence-and-audit/).
- **Evidence certificate (certificado de evidencia).** The sealed PDF bb-sign issues when an envelope
  completes, with its documents and fingerprints, its signers, the timeline and the integrity of the
  audit trail. It is for the organization; signers do not receive it. See
  [Evidence certificate](/en/sign/concepts/evidence-certificate/).
- **Frozen audit trail (registro de auditoría congelado).** The envelope's audit trail in a JSON file,
  fixed when the evidence certificate is issued and downloadable with it. In the application: "Audit
  trail (JSON)".
- **Evidence language (idioma de la evidencia).** The language, Spanish or English, in which an
  organization's evidence certificates are issued. Binary Bridges sets it.
- **Verify (verificación).** The public check, through the seal's QR code, that the seal matches what
  bb-sign recorded: the certificate that sealed and, for version 2 seals, the documents'
  fingerprints. See [Verify a signed document](/en/sign/signing/verify-a-document/).
- **Check a file (comprobar un archivo).** The public check that takes a PDF, computes its
  fingerprint and looks it up among the documents signed by bb-sign. The file is not stored. See
  [Verify a signed document](/en/sign/signing/verify-a-document/).

## Retention

- **Retention period (plazo de conservación).** How long bb-sign keeps each file before deleting it.
  In the envelope detail: "No longer available from". See
  [Scope and retention](/en/sign/legal/retention/).
- **File archiving (archivo de un archivo).** Moving a file to cheaper storage, from which it only
  downloads after a restore. The terms of service allow it, but today no file is moved. It is not the
  same as the Archived view.
- **Restore (restauración).** The request to download an archived file again. The restored copy stays
  available for <Fact of={RESTORE.days} unit="days" /> ("restored, available until"){RESTORE.requestableAgainAfterWindow.value && '; after that it can be requested again'}. See
  [Retention and restore](/en/sign/sending/retention-and-restore/).

## Integration

- **API credential (credencial de API).** An ID and a secret a system uses to call the API on the
  organization's behalf. The secret is shown once. See
  [API credentials](/en/sign/admin/api-credentials/).
- **Webhook.** An HTTPS URL of your organization to which bb-sign sends a signed notice when one of
  its {WEBHOOK_EVENTS.length} events happens: envelope sent, completed, cancelled or expired; signer
  viewed or signed; evidence certificate issued; file restored. See
  [Webhooks](/en/sign/developers/webhooks/).
- **Delivery (entrega).** Each notice bb-sign sends to a webhook for an event. See
  [Statuses](/en/sign/reference/statuses/).

## Certificates and entities

- **ECD (Entidad de Certificación Digital).** An entity accredited in Colombia to issue digital
  certificates. The organization's seal certificate is purchased from an ECD.
- **ONAC (Organismo Nacional de Acreditación de Colombia).** The body that accredits ECDs. Its
  directory lists the accredited entities.
- **AATL (Adobe Approved Trust List).** The list of issuers Adobe Acrobat trusts by default. Outside
  Colombia, the seal certificate is purchased from one of its members.
- **PKCS#12 (.p12, .pfx).** The file format that packages a certificate, its chain and the private
  key protected by a password. It is the format bb-sign uses to seal.
- **Certificate chain (cadena de certificación).** The organization's certificate together with the
  intermediate certificates and the root that back it. bb-sign embeds in every seal the chain the
  certificate file contains. See [Electronic signature and seal](/en/sign/concepts/signature-and-seal/).
- **Chain status (estado de la cadena).** The result of the chain check when the certificate is
  uploaded: **Complete chain** (`COMPLETE`), **No root** (`NO_ROOT`), **Leaf only** (`LEAF_ONLY`) or
  **Self-signed** (`SELF_SIGNED`). Certificates uploaded before that check show **Not checked**. See
  [Statuses](/en/sign/reference/statuses/).
- **Testing certificate (certificado de pruebas).** Binary Bridges' self-signed certificate that
  bb-sign seals with until the organization configures its own. PDF readers show it as untrusted. In
  the application: "Default testing certificate".
