The invitation to sign arrives by email and contains your personal signing link. bb-sign also
emails you when it is your turn, when your signature is pending and when the document is complete.

## Sender

Every email about documents comes from <Fact of={URLS.senderAddress} />. The signing page is
available in English and Spanish; you can switch the language in the top right corner.

## Emails you can receive

| Subject | When it arrives |
|---|---|
| `You have a document to sign: {title}` | When someone sends you a document. It contains your signing link. |
| `It's your turn to sign: {title}` | When the envelope has a signing order and the people before you have signed. It contains your signing link. |
| `Your verification code for BB-Sign` | When you request the verification code on the signing page. |
| `Reminder: please sign {title}` | When your signature is still pending. It contains the same link as the invitation. |
| `Signing complete: {title}` | When everyone has signed. It contains the link to your signed copy. |
| `Signing request expired: {title}` | When the envelope expired without your signature. |

`{title}` is the title given by the person who sent the document.

## Reminders

If your signature is still pending <Fact of={REMINDERS.thresholdHours} unit="hours" /> after the
invitation, bb-sign sends you a reminder. After that it sends at most one every
<Fact of={REMINDERS.cooldownHours} unit="hours" />, until you sign or the envelope closes.

## If you cannot find the email

1. Search your mailbox for `You have a document to sign` or <Fact of={URLS.senderAddress} />,
   including the spam folder.
2. If the email is in spam, mark it as safe so the next ones reach your inbox.
3. If you cannot find it, ask the person who sent you the document to confirm your email address
   on the envelope. They can correct it or send you a new envelope.

## Using the link

<Aside type="caution" title="Your link is personal">
  The link identifies you: whoever opens it signs with your name and email. Do not forward the
  email. If someone else must sign, ask the person who sent the document to add them as a signer.
</Aside>

The link is valid for <Fact of={ENVELOPE.linkTtlHoursDefault} unit="hours" /> by default, or for
the period set by the person who sent the document. Once you have signed, the same link takes you
to your signed copy.
