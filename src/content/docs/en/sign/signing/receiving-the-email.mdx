It all starts with an email. This page says how to recognize it, which other emails can follow
and what to do if you cannot find it.

## Who sends it

Every email about documents comes from <Fact of={URLS.senderAddress} />. Today they are written
in English; the signing page they lead to is in English and Spanish, and you can switch the
language at the top right.

## The emails you can receive

| Subject | When it arrives |
|---|---|
| `You have a document to sign: {title}` | When someone sends you a document. It contains your signing link. |
| `It's your turn to sign: {title}` | When the envelope has a signing order and the people before you have signed. It also contains your link. |
| `Your verification code for BB-Sign` | When you request the verification code on the signing page. |
| `Reminder: please sign {title}` | When you have gone a while without signing. It carries the same link as the invitation. |
| `Signing complete: {title}` | When everyone has signed. Its link leads to your signed copy. |
| `Signing request expired: {title}` | When the envelope expired before you signed. It can no longer be signed. |

`{title}` is the title given by whoever sent the document.

## Reminders

If you have not signed <Fact of={REMINDERS.thresholdHours} unit="hours" /> after the invitation,
bb-sign sends you a reminder, and then at most one every
<Fact of={REMINDERS.cooldownHours} unit="hours" />, until you sign or the envelope closes.

## If you cannot find the email

1. Search your mailbox for `You have a document to sign` or <Fact of={URLS.senderAddress} />,
   including the spam or junk folder.
2. If it is in spam, mark it as safe so the next ones reach your inbox.
3. If it is not there, ask whoever sent it to check on the envelope that your address is spelled
   correctly. Today there is no button to resend you the link; the sender sees the state of the
   envelope and can correct it or create another one.

## The link is personal

<Aside type="caution" title="Do not forward the email">
  The link identifies one person: you. Whoever opens it signs with your name and your email, and
  that signature cannot be undone. If someone else must sign, ask whoever sent the document to add
  them as a signer.
</Aside>

The link is valid for <Fact of={ENVELOPE.linkTtlHoursDefault} unit="hours" /> by default (the
sender may set a different period) and stops working for signing the moment you sign. If you open
it again afterwards, you see your signed copy, not the signing page.
