Someone sent you a document to sign with bb-sign. You need no account and no password: everything
happens in the link that arrives in your email, and it works on a phone. There are six steps and
most take a couple of minutes.

<Steps>

1. **The email arrives.** The subject is `You have a document to sign: {title}` and it comes from
   <Fact of={URLS.senderAddress} />. If you cannot find it, check your spam folder.

2. **Open the link.** It is valid for <Fact of={ENVELOPE.linkTtlHoursDefault} unit="hours" /> by
   default; whoever sent the document may have set a different period. The link is personal: do
   not forward it. When it opens you see "Signing as" with your name and your email.

3. **Review every document.** Under "Documents to Review", press "View" on each one. The
   application does not let you continue until you have opened them all; if you try earlier, it
   says "Open every document before you continue." When you are done, press "I have reviewed the
   document".

4. **Verify your identity, if asked.** On the "Verify Your Identity" screen you receive by email
   a code of <Fact of={OTP.digits} unit="digits" />, valid for
   <Fact of={OTP.validitySeconds} unit="s" />. Type it and press "Verify & Continue". If the
   envelope does not require this step, you go straight to signing.

5. **Sign.** On "Add Your Signature" choose "Draw" (with a finger or the mouse) or "Type" (your
   full name). Press "Submit Signature". A submitted signature cannot be undone.

6. **Download your copy.** The "Document Signed Successfully!" screen shows the button "Download
   Your Signed Document" as soon as the copy is ready. If other people must sign too, the copy is
   prepared once everyone has signed and you receive the email `Signing complete: {title}` with a
   link to download it.

</Steps>

## If something does not go as described

Every message the application can show you is in
[If something goes wrong](/en/sign/signing/troubleshooting/), with what happened and what you can
do. Two things do not exist today: declining to sign (if you do not want to sign, do not sign and
tell whoever sent it) and asking for a new link (ask whoever sent you the document).
