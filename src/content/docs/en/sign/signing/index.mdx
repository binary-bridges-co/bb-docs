You can sign the document you were sent directly from the link in your email, on a computer or a
phone. You need no account or password, and the process takes a few minutes.

<Steps>

1. **Open the email.** It comes from <Fact of={URLS.senderAddress} /> with the subject
   `You have a document to sign: {title}`. If it is not in your inbox, check your spam folder.

2. **Open the link.** The link is valid for
   <Fact of={ENVELOPE.linkTtlHoursDefault} unit="hours" /> by default, or for the period set by
   the person who sent you the document. It is personal: only you should use it. When it opens you
   see **Signing as** with your name and email.

3. **Review the documents.** Under **Documents to Review**, press **View** on each document. Next to
   each one you see its SHA-256 fingerprint ("Fingerprint (SHA-256):"). Once you have opened them all,
   press **I have reviewed the document**.

4. **Verify your identity, if requested.** You receive a code of
   <Fact of={OTP.digits} unit="digits" /> by email, valid for
   <Fact of={OTP.validityMinutes} unit="minutes" />. Enter it on **Verify Your Identity** and
   press **Verify & Continue**.

5. **Sign.** On **Add Your Signature**, choose **Draw** to trace your signature, **Type** to
   enter your full name or **Upload image** to use a photo or scan of your signature, and press
   **Submit Signature**. A submitted signature is final.

6. **Download your copy.** When the sealed copy is ready, press
   **Download Your Signed Document** and, once it appears, **Download Your Evidence Certificate**. If
   other people must also sign, you receive the email `Signing complete: {title}` with the download
   link once everyone has signed.

</Steps>

## If you need help

[If something goes wrong](/en/sign/signing/troubleshooting/) lists every message the signing page
can show and what to do in each case. For a new link, or if you do not wish to sign, contact the
person who sent you the document.
