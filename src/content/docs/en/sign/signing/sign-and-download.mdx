The "Add Your Signature" screen is the last step. This page explains the two ways to sign, what
bb-sign keeps at that moment and how the signed copy reaches you.

## Draw or type

- **"Draw"**: trace your signature with a finger, a stylus or the mouse in the box "Draw your
  signature here". "Clear" wipes it so you can start again.
- **"Type"**: type your name in "Type your full name". The application turns it into an image
  with a handwriting font.

In both cases what is kept is an image of your signature. There is no way to upload an image made
elsewhere. If you press "Submit Signature" with the box empty, you see "Please draw your
signature." or "Please type your name.".

## What is recorded

When you press "Submit Signature", bb-sign keeps, next to your name and your email:

- the image of your signature;
- the date and time;
- the IP address you signed from;
- the browser you used.

These details are part of the envelope's evidence. Your name, your email and the date also appear
on the seal printed on the document.

<Aside type="caution" title="Submitting the signature cannot be undone">
  As soon as the signature is recorded, your link is used up and the envelope moves on. If you
  signed by mistake or the details were not yours, tell whoever sent you the document: only that
  person can cancel the envelope or create another one.
</Aside>

## The final screen

After submitting you see "Document Signed Successfully!". The line below it is written by the
server: "Signature recorded successfully" if other people still have to sign, or "All signers
have signed. Envelope completed!" if you were the last. Then one of these states:

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| "Download Your Signed Document" | The sealed copy is ready. There is one button per document. |
| "Preparing your signed copy — this can take a few moments." | Everyone has signed and bb-sign is sealing the PDFs. The page refreshes on its own. |
| "Your signed copy is still being prepared. We have emailed you a link — you can download it from there once it is ready." | Sealing is taking longer than usual. You can close the page; the completion email carries the link. |
| No button | Other people still have to sign. The copy will exist once everyone has signed. |

## The completion email

When everyone has signed you receive `Signing complete: {title}` with a link to your copy. That
link opens the final screen with "This envelope is complete. You can download your signed copy
below." You can come back to it as often as you like while the copy is available.

## Until when you can download

The copy is available from your link until the envelope is archived, which happens
<Fact of={ENVELOPE.archiveAfterDays} unit="days" /> after it was created (not after you signed).
After that you see "This document is no longer available for download. Please contact the
administrator.": ask whoever sent you the document, who still has access. Save your copy as soon
as you download it.

## What the seal shows

On every signed document, where the sender placed it or at the end of the last page, there is a
seal with: the header "Verified Signature" and the envelope identifier, your signature, your
name, your email, the date ("Signed" and the day as year-month-day, in UTC), a QR code and a
circular pattern generated from the signature's fingerprint. The PDF also carries the
organization's certificate seal, visible in the PDF reader's signature panel. What the QR checks
is in [Verify a signed document](/en/sign/signing/verify-a-document/); the difference between
your signature and the certificate seal, in
[Electronic signature and seal](/en/sign/concepts/signature-and-seal/).
