bb-sign records everything that happens to each envelope, from creation to archiving: who sent it,
who opened the link, who signed, when and from which IP address. Every event is chained to the
previous one, so any alteration of the record is detected. When the envelope completes, that
evidence is also delivered as an evidence certificate in PDF.

**Who:** organization admins and platform admins, and the Auditors, Contributors and Managers of the
envelope's workspace. **Where:** the **Audit Trail** section of each envelope's detail page.

## Review an envelope's trail

On the envelope detail, the **Audit Trail** section lists every event with its type, who caused it,
the date and time, and its hash. Document and signature events also show the SHA-256 hashes they
recorded, for example "Original SHA-256: …".

The Auditors, Contributors and Managers of the envelope's workspace can review it, as can every
admin. Someone without that permission sees "The audit trail is available to auditors and managers of
this workspace." For a Viewer to review it, give them the Auditor role in
[Workspaces and members](/en/sign/admin/workspaces-and-members/).

### Who appears as the author

| Author | How it is shown |
| --- | --- |
| A person in your organization | Their name or, if they have none, their email. With neither, "Unknown user". |
| An API credential | The credential's label and client ID, with the **API** badge. With neither, "API credential". |
| A signer | Their name or their email. |
| bb-sign | "System", for automatic actions. |
| No recorded author | "Actor not recorded", when the event arrived without its author. |

### Envelope events

| Event | Name in the app | When it is recorded |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ENVELOPE_CREATED` | Envelope created | Someone created the envelope. |
| `DOCUMENT_ADDED` | Document added | A document was added; the SHA-256 of the original is recorded. |
| `ENVELOPE_SENT` | Envelope sent | Someone sent the envelope to its signers. |
| `EMAIL_SENT`, `EMAIL_DELIVERED`, `EMAIL_OPENED`, `EMAIL_BOUNCED` | | An envelope email was sent, delivered, opened or bounced. |
| `SIGNING_LINK_CLICKED` | | A signer opened their signing link. |
| `SIGNING_VIEW_OPENED`, `SIGNING_VIEW_CLOSED` | | A signer opened or closed the document view. |
| `SIGNER_ELIGIBLE` | | In sequential signing, a signer's turn came. |
| `OTP_REQUESTED`, `OTP_VERIFIED`, `OTP_FAILED`, `OTP_LOCKED` | | A signer requested a one-time code, verified it, typed a wrong one or ran out of attempts. |
| `SIGNER_SIGNED` | Signer signed | A signer signed; their IP address, identification method and the hash of the document set they signed are recorded. |
| `ENVELOPE_COMPLETED` | Envelope completed | The last signer signed. |
| `DOCUMENTS_SEALED` | Signed documents sealed | The signed PDFs were sealed. |
| `EVIDENCE_CERTIFICATE_ISSUED`, `EVIDENCE_CERTIFICATE_FAILED` | Evidence certificate issued, Evidence certificate could not be generated | The evidence certificate was issued, or could not be generated. |
| `EVIDENCE_CERTIFICATE_ACCESSED` | Evidence certificate downloaded | Someone downloaded the evidence certificate. |
| `EVIDENCE_CERTIFICATE_REISSUE_REQUESTED` | Evidence certificate re-issued by platform staff | Binary Bridges re-issued a certificate that could not be generated. |
| `SIGNED_COPY_ACCESSED` | | A signer downloaded their signed copy from their link. |
| `ENVELOPE_CANCELLED`, `ENVELOPE_EXPIRED` | Envelope cancelled, Envelope expired | Someone cancelled the envelope, or it expired before completion. |
| `ENVELOPE_ARCHIVED` | Envelope archived | The envelope moved to the **Archived** view. |
| `AUDIT_TRAIL_EXPORTED` | | Someone exported the envelope's trail through the API. |

Events without a name in the app are shown with their code.

## Organization configuration changes

bb-sign also records, with who did it, the configuration changes that affect your organization:

- Creating and revoking API credentials.
- Creating a webhook, rotating its secret and deleting it.
- Creating, renaming and deleting workspaces.
- Granting and removing workspace roles for people and API keys.
- Uploading and removing the organization's certificate, and each notice of its expiry.
- Publishing agreement texts.

These events do not appear on an envelope's detail page. If you need them, request them from Binary
Bridges support.

## How the record is protected

Every event belongs to one chain: its envelope's, its organization's or the platform-wide one. Within
each chain events carry a consecutive number with no gaps, and each one's SHA-256 hash covers its
content, its position and the previous event's hash. Modifying, inserting or deleting an event
therefore breaks the chain from that point on and makes the alteration evident.

The record only accepts new events: no database user can modify or delete an event. bb-sign verifies
every chain each night, at <Fact of={AUDIT.nightlyVerifyTime} /> (time zone
<Fact of={AUDIT.nightlyVerifyTimeZone} />).

## Download an envelope's evidence

When the envelope completes, bb-sign issues its evidence certificate: a sealed PDF stating who
signed, how they were identified, when and which documents, together with a fixed copy of the audit
trail up to that moment. On the envelope detail, the **Evidence certificate** row offers:

- **Evidence certificate (PDF)**, the sealed certificate.
- **Audit trail (JSON)**, the copy of the trail that comes with the certificate.

Anyone who can download the envelope's documents
(<code><Fact of={EVIDENCE.downloadPermission} /></code>) can download them: Auditors, Contributors,
Managers and admins. Each download creates a link valid for
<Fact of={EVIDENCE.downloadLinkMinutes} unit="minutes" /> and is recorded in the trail as
`EVIDENCE_CERTIFICATE_ACCESSED`.

| The row shows | What happened and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Evidence certificate processing..." | bb-sign is issuing the certificate. The page updates by itself when it is ready. |
| "It could not be generated; the team was notified." | Issuing failed and Binary Bridges already knows; it can re-issue it within <Fact of={EVIDENCE.reissueWindowDays} unit="days" /> of the failure, while the envelope is not archived. If you need it, write to support before then. |
| "Completed before evidence certificates were enabled." | The envelope completed before certificates existed. Use the signed documents and the audit trail on the page. |
| "The retention period has ended and the certificate was deleted." | The <Fact of={EVIDENCE.retentionDays} unit="days" /> of retention have passed. Use the copy your organization kept. |
| "The evidence certificate is available to those who can download this envelope's documents." | You do not have that permission in the envelope's workspace. Ask an admin for the Auditor role or higher. |

An integration can get the same through the API, and export an envelope's full trail with
<code><Fact of={AUDIT.exportPath} /></code> (permission `audit:read`, format
<code><Fact of={AUDIT.exportFormat} /></code>). Each export is recorded in the envelope's trail.
