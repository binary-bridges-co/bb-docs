**Who:** organization admin (platform admins too). **Where:** Users (`/users`) in the sidebar.

Every person who uses bb-sign has an account in your organization. You create it; the person
receives an email, sets a password and signs in. There is no self-registration.

## Invite a person

<Steps>

1. Press "New User". The "Create User" form opens.

2. Enter "Username", "Email" and, if you want, "First Name" and "Last Name". The email is the
   person's identity: the invitation goes there and they sign in with it.

3. Choose the "Role". The "Organization" field already holds yours and cannot be changed.

4. Press "Create User". You see "User created successfully", and the form reminds you that "User
   will receive an email to set their password".

</Steps>

If the account was created but the email did not go out, you see "User created, but the set-up
email could not be sent. Use "Resend invitation" to try again". The account exists; do not create it
again.

## Roles you may assign

| Role in the list | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| "Org Member" (`org_member`) | Create, edit and send envelopes, upload documents and set labels in the workspaces where they are Contributor or above. In General they always are. They cannot cancel envelopes. |
| "Org Admin" (`org_admin`) | Everything in this section: users, workspaces, labels, credentials, webhooks, certificate. They see every envelope in every workspace. |

You can never create a "Platform Admin": that option appears only in a platform admin's list. Nor
can you invite someone into another organization: the "Organization" field is fixed to yours. Each
permission is detailed in the [Permissions matrix](/en/sign/admin/permissions/).

## The invitation email and its link

The identity server sends the invitation from <code>{URLS.identitySenderAddress.value}</code>, with a
link to set the password. The link is valid for <Fact of={AUTH.invitationLinkHours} unit="hours" />.
After that the link stops working and the invitation must be resent; the account still exists.

## Resend an invitation

Every row in the list has a "Resend invitation" button. Pressing it sends the person a new email
with a new link. You see "Invitation email sent" or, if it failed, "Could not send the invitation
email": wait a moment and try again.

Use it when the link expired, when the email got lost or when the person cannot find it.

## Active and disabled

Every row shows "Active" or "Disabled". A disabled account cannot sign in. Today the app has no
switch to disable or re-enable someone; ask Binary Bridges support.

## What a member sees and what an admin sees

| Sidebar entry | Member | Organization admin | Platform admin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dashboard, Envelopes, Archived, New Envelope | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Labels, Users, Storage, Settings | No | Yes | Yes |
| Organizations | No | No | Yes |

A member who opens an admin address sees "Access Denied" and "Your role does not have permission to
view this page". What a member can do with an envelope depends on their role in the envelope's
workspace; see [Workspaces and members](/en/sign/admin/workspaces-and-members/).

<Aside type="note" title="User limit">
  If your quota has a maximum number of users and you reached it, creating one shows "Your
  organization has reached its user limit". Ask the platform admin to raise it; see
  [Quota and usage](/en/sign/admin/quota-and-usage/).
</Aside>
