You create the account of every person who uses bb-sign in your organization. The person receives
an email, sets a password and signs in with their email address. Only the people you invite have
access.

**Who:** organization admins and platform admins. **Where:** **Users**, in the sidebar.

## Invite a person

<Steps>

1. Press **New User**. The **Create User** form opens.

2. Enter the **Username** and **Email** and, optionally, the **First Name** and **Last Name**. The
   email identifies the person: the invitation goes there and they sign in with it.

3. Choose the **Role**. The **Organization** field already shows yours.

4. Press **Create User**. You see "User created successfully" and the person receives the email to
   set their password.

</Steps>

If "User created, but the set-up email could not be sent" appears, the account already exists. Use
**Resend invitation** in its row instead of creating it again.

## Choose the role

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Org Member** | Create, edit and send envelopes, upload documents and set labels in the workspaces where they are Contributor or Manager. In the General workspace they are always a Contributor. Cancelling envelopes is for Managers and admins. |
| **Org Admin** | Every task in this section: users, workspaces, labels, credentials, webhooks, certificate and agreements. They see every envelope in every workspace. They sign in with a second factor. |

The accounts you create always belong to your organization. Each permission is detailed in the
[Permissions matrix](/en/sign/admin/permissions/).

## The invitation link

The invitation comes from <code>{URLS.identitySenderAddress.value}</code> with a link to set the
password, valid for <Fact of={AUTH.invitationLinkHours} unit="hours" />. If it expires, resend the
invitation; the account remains. The password must have at least
<Fact of={AUTH.passwordMinLength} /> characters and cannot be the username or the email.

If the invitation is for an admin, after the password the person sets up an authenticator app on their
phone, and from then on uses it at every sign-in. Members do not have this step. The details are in
[Sign-in security](/en/sign/admin/sign-in-security/).

## Resend an invitation

Every row in the list has a **Resend invitation** button. The person receives a new email with a
new link, and you see "Invitation email sent". Use it when the link has expired or the person cannot
find the email.

If "Could not send the invitation email" appears, wait a moment and try again.

## Active and disabled accounts

Every row shows **Active** or **Disabled**. A disabled account cannot sign in. To disable or
re-enable an account, ask Binary Bridges support.

## What each role sees in the menu

| Sidebar entry | Member | Organization admin | Platform admin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Dashboard**, **Envelopes**, **Archived**, **New Envelope** | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Labels**, **Users**, **Storage**, **Settings** | No | Yes | Yes |
| **Organizations** | No | No | Yes |

If a member opens an admin page, they see "Access Denied". What a member can do with an envelope
depends on their role in the envelope's workspace; see
[Workspaces and members](/en/sign/admin/workspaces-and-members/).

<Aside type="note" title="User limit">
  If your organization has a maximum number of users and has reached it, creating an account shows
  "Your organization has reached its user limit". Ask Binary Bridges to raise it; see
  [Quota and usage](/en/sign/admin/quota-and-usage/).
</Aside>
