Workspaces let you decide who sees each envelope. Every envelope belongs to one workspace, and its
members and the admins see it. You can, for example, keep employment contracts to the HR team.

**Who:** organization admins and platform admins. **Where:** **Settings**, **Workspaces** tab.

## The General workspace

Every organization starts with the General workspace. All members of the organization are
Contributors in General automatically: they can create and send envelopes there. General is
permanent, so it is not renamed or deleted.

Create another workspace when you want a smaller group to see certain envelopes.

## Create, rename and delete a workspace

<Steps>

1. Press **New workspace**, enter the **Name** and press **Create workspace**. Each workspace needs
   a distinct name.

2. To change the name, press **Rename** in the row and enter the **New name**.

3. To delete an empty workspace, press **Delete** and confirm with **Delete workspace**. Deletion is
   permanent.

</Steps>

A workspace can be deleted when it holds no envelopes, active or archived. Envelopes stay in the
workspace where they were created, so a workspace that has held envelopes is kept. If you try to
delete it, the app offers the **View its envelopes** link.

## Add members

Workspace members can be people or API keys. Press **Members** in the workspace's row.

<Steps>

1. Under **Add member**, open **Select…**. People appear with their name and email, and credentials
   with their label followed by "(API key)".

2. Choose the role and press **Add**.

3. To change a member's role, use the role selector in their row. To remove them, press **Remove**
   and confirm.

</Steps>

Admins see every workspace without being added, which is why they do not appear in the member list.

Creating, renaming and deleting each workspace, and every role granted or removed, are recorded in
the [audit trail](/en/sign/admin/audit-trail/) with who did it.

## Workspace roles

| Role | What it allows in the workspace |
| --- | --- |
| Viewer | See the envelopes and the archive. |
| Auditor | Everything a Viewer can do, plus read the audit trail and download documents. |
| Manager | Create, edit, send and cancel envelopes, upload documents, set labels, download and read the audit trail. |

Contributor is the role every member has in General. For someone to create and send envelopes in
another workspace, make them Manager. The full table is in the
[Permissions matrix](/en/sign/admin/permissions/).

## Limits and timing

- A person or API key can belong to up to <Fact of={AUTH.maxWorkspaceMembershipsPerPrincipal} />
  workspaces.
- Access changes apply when the person's or integration's session refreshes, within
  <Fact of={AUTH.tokenLifetimeSeconds} unit="s" />. This holds both for granting access and for
  removing it.
- If you see "Access for this workspace is still being set up. Try again in a moment", wait a few
  seconds and repeat the action.

<Aside type="tip" title="API keys">
  Every API credential needs access to at least one workspace. You can assign it when you create
  the credential or add it as a member later; see [API credentials](/en/sign/admin/api-credentials/).
</Aside>
