bb-sign keeps each of your organization's files for a fixed period, the same for every organization.
The **Storage** page shows you the service's policy and the rules the storage actually applies. For
organization admins it is read-only.

**Who:** organization admins and platform admins. **Where:** **Storage**, in the sidebar.

## What the page shows

The **Storage Lifecycle** page has two parts:

- **Configuration** shows the product's declared policy: **Standard-IA Transition**, **Glacier
  Transition** and **Object Expiration**, in days. The values are the same for every organization.
- **Bucket Rules** shows the rules each storage bucket actually has. The organization's certificates
  are excluded: they do not expire.

The two parts are shown separately so you can see whether the storage applies something different
from the policy. Today, in production, the documents storage has a single rule:
<Fact of={RETENTION_IMPLEMENTED.documentsExpirationDays} unit="days" /> of retention, with no
transitions. No file moves to archive storage today.

## How long your files are kept

| Item | Service policy | Today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retention | <Fact of={RETENTION_POLICY.retentionDays} unit="days" /> per file: the original from upload, the signed document from the envelope's completion and the evidence certificate from issue. After that, the file is deleted permanently. | Applied as stated. |
| Move to archive storage | Documents older than <Fact of={RETENTION_POLICY.archiveAfterDays} unit="days" /> may move to archive storage, and then need a restore before they can be downloaded. | No file is moved. |
| Restore | An archived copy is restored on request, from the envelope page, through the API or by the signer from their link. The restored copy stays available for <Fact of={RESTORE.days} unit="days" />. | Available, unused while nothing is archived. |
| Evidence certificate and audit trail | Never archived: they download immediately throughout their <Fact of={EVIDENCE.retentionDays} unit="days" />. | Applied as stated. |

The period is fixed and is not configured per organization. The terms that set it are in section 3
of the [Terms and conditions](/en/sign/legal/terms/).

## Retention dates of each envelope

The **Retention** section of each envelope's detail page shows "No longer available from …": from
that date, at 00:00 UTC, the envelope's files can no longer be downloaded. The "Archived from …" date
only appears when automatic archiving is active, and is not shown today. See
[Retention and restore](/en/sign/sending/retention-and-restore/).

<Aside type="note" title="The Archived view is not archive storage">
  <Fact of={ENVELOPE.archiveAfterDays} unit="days" /> after creation, a completed, cancelled or
  expired envelope moves to the **Archived** view. It is only a change of list: the envelope and its
  files can still be downloaded, including from the signer's link, until their retention period
  ends.
</Aside>

## Save your copies within the period

<Aside type="caution" title="When the period ends, retention is your organization's">
  Download and archive the signed documents and evidence certificates your organization must keep
  before the <Fact of={RETENTION_POLICY.retentionDays} unit="days" /> period ends. The recommended
  way, automatic as each envelope completes, is in the
  [Retention and restore guide](/en/sign/legal/retention-guide/).
</Aside>
