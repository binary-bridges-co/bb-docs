**Who:** organization admin, read-only (platform admins can also change it). **Where:** Storage
(`/storage/lifecycle`) in the sidebar.

The "Storage Lifecycle" page shows the rules the application applies to stored files: when they move
to cheaper storage tiers and when they expire. What you see is service configuration, the same for
every organization.

## What the page shows

"Configuration" holds three values:

| Field | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| "Standard-IA Transition" "(AWS S3 only)" | Days after which a file moves to an infrequent-access tier. It reads the same. |
| "Glacier Transition" "(AWS S3 only)" | Days after which a file moves to cold storage. Retrieving it is no longer immediate. |
| "Object Expiration" | Days after which the rule deletes the file. |

"Bucket Rules" lists every "managed" bucket with its rules and, when one expires, "expires after
N d". The certificates bucket is "excluded": "No lifecycle rules — certificates do not expire".

## Where those numbers come from

The "Configuration" values are the application's lifecycle defaults, with an expiration of
<Fact of={RETENTION_IMPLEMENTED.nonProdLifecycleDefaultDays} unit="days" />. They apply in the
environments that are not production. In production, storage is governed by the infrastructure, not
by this page.

## What production does today

| What | Today in production |
| --- | --- |
| Move to Standard-IA | After <Fact of={RETENTION_IMPLEMENTED.documentsStandardIaDays} unit="days" /> |
| Move to Glacier (cold storage) | After <Fact of={RETENTION_IMPLEMENTED.documentsGlacierDays} unit="days" /> |
| Expiration rule | None. Today no process deletes signed documents. |

This describes what the infrastructure does right now, not what the service promises. The promise is
in the terms: a fixed retention of <Fact of={RETENTION_POLICY.retentionDays} unit="days" /> and,
after that, conservation is your organization's responsibility.

<Aside type="caution" title="Do not mistake the page for the policy">
  That no expiration rule exists today does not mean documents are kept forever. The period that
  counts is the one in the terms of service. Keep your copies in time; the recommended way is in
  [Scope and retention](/en/sign/legal/retention/).
</Aside>

## Who can change it

An organization admin sees the page read-only. Only a platform admin sees the editable "Object
Expiration" field and the "Apply" button. The Standard-IA and Glacier transitions are not edited from
the app. There is no per-organization configuration: retention is not negotiable.
