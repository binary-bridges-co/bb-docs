bb-sign keeps your organization's documents for a fixed retention period and moves them over time
to lower-cost storage tiers. The **Storage** page shows you those rules in read-only mode.

**Who:** organization admins, read-only; platform admins can change it. **Where:** **Storage**, in
the sidebar.

## What the page shows

The **Storage Lifecycle** page has two parts:

- **Configuration** shows, in days, when files move to lower-cost storage tiers and the **Object
  Expiration** value.
- The rules list shows which files they apply to. The organization's certificates are excluded: they
  do not expire.

This configuration is the same for every organization.

## How long your documents are kept

| Stage | When |
| --- | --- |
| Retention of signed documents | <Fact of={RETENTION_POLICY.retentionDays} unit="days" />, a fixed period, the same for every organization |
| Move to archive storage | After <Fact of={RETENTION_IMPLEMENTED.documentsGlacierDays} unit="days" /> |

Documents in archive storage are kept intact; retrieving them takes longer than a recent document.
When the retention period ends, keeping the documents is your organization's responsibility.

<Aside type="caution" title="Save your copies within the period">
  Download and archive the signed documents your organization must keep before the
  <Fact of={RETENTION_POLICY.retentionDays} unit="days" /> period ends. The recommended way is in
  [Scope and retention](/en/sign/legal/retention/).
</Aside>

## Who can change the configuration

Organization admins view the page. The Binary Bridges platform administrator is the one who can
change the **Object Expiration** with the **Apply** button. The retention period is fixed and is not
configured per organization.
