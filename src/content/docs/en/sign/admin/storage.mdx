Each organization's files are kept in storage dedicated to it, isolated from other customers and
encrypted. Each organization has its own retention policy: how long its files are kept and in which
storage tiers they spend that time.

**Who:** organization administrators (permission
<code>{ORG_STORAGE.managePermission.value}</code>). **Where:** **Settings**, **Storage** tab.

## The standard configuration

An organization starts with the standard configuration:

- Each file is kept <Fact of={ORG_STORAGE.defaultRetentionDays} unit="days" /> and then deleted for
  good. The period is counted per file: the original from upload, the signed document from the moment
  the envelope completes and the evidence certificate from issue.
- After <Fact of={ORG_STORAGE.defaultCheaperTierAfterDays} unit="days" />, files move to a cheaper
  storage tier that still has "Instant access": with the standard configuration, no file ever needs a
  restore before it downloads.

## Changing the configuration

The period can run from <Fact of={ORG_STORAGE.minRetentionDays} unit="days" /> to
<Fact of={ORG_STORAGE.maxRetentionYears} unit="years" />, and the policy can add cheaper storage tiers
as files age. The minimum protects the documents of envelopes still being signed.

The **Storage** tab shows the current policy, read-only: how long files are kept, a timeline with each
tier and the earlier versions of the policy. To change it:

1. Click **Request a change**, describe the policy you need and explain why, in
   <Fact of={ORG_STORAGE.requestReasonMin} /> to <Fact of={ORG_STORAGE.requestReasonMax} /> characters.
2. Binary Bridges reviews the request and approves it as requested, adjusts and approves it, or rejects
   it. When it adjusts or rejects a request, it leaves a note that says why.
3. The decision, with its note, shows in the same tab. When a request is approved, the new policy shows
   as one more version.

Your organization can have one pending request at a time. While it waits for a decision, you can
withdraw it with **Withdraw** and send another.

## Availability

Each tier of the policy says whether its files download right away:

| What the tier shows | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| "Instant access" | Files download directly. |
| "Needs a restore, ready in hours" | A file has to be restored before it downloads. In the deepest tier, a restore takes up to about <Fact of={ORG_STORAGE.deepestRestoreMaxHours} unit="hours" />. The restored copy can be downloaded for <Fact of={RESTORE.days} unit="days" />. |

Only the original of a completed envelope and the signed document can ever need a restore. Drafts
never do, and the evidence certificate and the audit trail always download right away. How to request
a restore, from the app, the API or the signer's link, is in
[Retention and restore](/en/sign/sending/retention-and-restore/).

## What protects each file

- **Evidence certificates and their audit trail.** They are locked against deletion until the end of
  their period, the one in force when the certificate was issued. Nobody can delete them earlier.
- **Original and signed documents.** They are kept with versions, not locked: they are kept for your
  organization's period and then deleted. Only a shorter period, applied by Binary Bridges, deletes them
  earlier (next section).

## Shortening the period

Only Binary Bridges applies a shorter period, when it approves or adjusts a request. Before it applies
one, the Binary Bridges reviewer sees how many documents would be deleted, including those of envelopes
still waiting for signatures, and until when each evidence certificate is kept.

Once it is applied, documents older than the new period are deleted within about
<Fact of={ORG_STORAGE.shortenDeletionDays} unit="days" /> and cannot be recovered. Evidence
certificates and their audit trail are kept until their date. If your organization asks for a shorter
period, download what it must keep first.

## While the storage is being prepared

While a new organization's storage is being prepared, the tab shows "Your storage is being prepared.
New uploads start once it is ready." and no document can be uploaded: the upload is refused and
nothing is stored. Try again in a minute. An integration receives the
[`STORAGE_NOT_READY`](/en/sign/developers/errors/) error.

## Files from before dedicated storage

Files your organization created before it had dedicated storage stay where they were and keep the
previous rule: <Fact of={ORG_STORAGE.legacyRetentionDays} unit="days" />. The **Storage** tab says from
which date your organization's policy applies.

## The evidence certificate states the period

Each evidence certificate states the retention period of the envelope's files: a single one, or one per
line when the files have different periods.

## Dates of each envelope

The **Retention** section of each envelope's detail shows "No longer available from …": from that date,
at 00:00 UTC, the envelope's files can no longer be downloaded. See
[Retention and restore](/en/sign/sending/retention-and-restore/).

<Aside type="note" title="The Archived view is not a storage tier">
  <Fact of={ENVELOPE.archiveAfterDays} unit="days" /> after it was created, a completed, cancelled or
  expired envelope moves to the **Archived** view. It is only a list change: the envelope and its files
  can still be downloaded, from the signer's link too, until their retention period ends.
</Aside>

## Keep your copies within the period

<Aside type="caution" title="When the period ends, keeping the files is your organization's job">
  Download and archive the signed documents and evidence certificates your organization must keep
  before your policy's period ends. The recommended way, automatic as each envelope completes, is in
  [Retention and restore](/en/sign/sending/retention-and-restore/).
</Aside>
