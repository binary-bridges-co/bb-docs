These terms form part of the service agreement signed between Binary Bridges S.A.S. and each customer. They are published in the application for reference; they do not require online acceptance. Every change increments the version and is recorded in the history at the end of this document.

## 1. Nature of the service

**1.1** bb-sign provides an electronic signature mechanism in the terms of Decreto 2364 de 2012 (compiled in Decreto 1074 de 2015). It is not a digital signature (*firma digital*) in the terms of article 28 of Ley 527 de 1999.

**1.2** Each customer, through its legal office, decides whether the mechanism is appropriate for the documents it sends for signature.

## 2. Seal certificate

**2.1** When an envelope completes, bb-sign seals each signed document with a digital certificate; any later alteration of the document is detectable.

**2.2** Until the customer configures a certificate of its own, bb-sign seals with a Binary Bridges **testing certificate**. It is self-signed, it does not give third-party trust, and validators (for example Adobe Acrobat or the European Commission's validator) will show it as untrusted.

**2.3** The customer is responsible for obtaining a certificate issued by a Digital Certification Entity accredited by ONAC, configuring it in bb-sign with its full certification chain, and renewing it before it expires. The guide is at [Your own certificate](/en/sign/admin/own-certificate/).

**2.4** The lack of a certificate of its own does not prevent sending envelopes; in that case the documents are sealed with the testing certificate.

**2.5** bb-sign stores the certificate file and its password encrypted, and uses them exclusively to seal that organization's documents.
