Signatures collected in bb-sign are electronic signatures (*firma electrónica*) with legal validity in
Colombia, under Ley 527 de 1999 and Decreto 2364 de 2012. This page gathers the applicable legal texts
and explains how bb-sign meets each condition: it identifies the signer, protects the integrity of the
document and keeps the evidence of every signature.

## Legal basis

The Spanish text is the authoritative one; a short summary follows each quotation.

### Ley 527 de 1999, article 7: the signature

> **Artículo 7. Firma.** Cuando cualquier norma exija la presencia de una firma o establezca ciertas
> consecuencias en ausencia de la misma, en relación con un mensaje de datos, se entenderá
> satisfecho dicho requerimiento si:
>
> a) Se ha utilizado un método que permita identificar al iniciador de un mensaje de datos y para
> indicar que el contenido cuenta con su aprobación;
>
> b) Que el método sea tanto confiable como apropiado para el propósito por el cual el mensaje fue
> generado o comunicado.
>
> Lo dispuesto en este artículo se aplicará tanto si el requisito establecido en cualquier norma
> constituye una obligación, como si las normas simplemente prevén consecuencias en el caso de que
> no exista una firma.

The law sets two conditions: the method identifies the signer and shows their approval of the
content, and it is reliable and appropriate for the purpose. It does not require a specific
technology.

### Decreto 2364 de 2012: the electronic signature

Decreto 2364 de 2012, compiled in Decreto 1074 de 2015, regulates article 7 of Ley 527 and defines
the electronic signature as a method (codes, passwords, biometric data, private cryptographic keys or
others) that identifies a person in relation to a data message, provided it is reliable and
appropriate for the purposes it is used for, considering all the circumstances of the case and any
agreement between the parties.

The decree treats the signature as reliable when the signature creation data corresponds exclusively
to the signer and any later alteration of the message is detectable. An electronic signature has the
same value as a handwritten one; whoever relies on it demonstrates its validity through the evidence
of the method.

### Ley 527 de 1999, article 28: the digital signature

> **Artículo 28. Atributos jurídicos de una firma digital.** Cuando una firma digital haya sido fijada
> en un mensaje de datos se presume que el suscriptor de aquella tenía la intención de acreditar ese
> mensaje de datos y de ser vinculado con el contenido del mismo.
>
> **Parágrafo.** El uso de una firma digital tendrá la misma fuerza y efectos que el uso de una firma
> manuscrita, si aquélla incorpora los siguientes atributos:
>
> 1. Es única a la persona que la usa.
> 2. Es susceptible de ser verificada.
> 3. Está bajo el control exclusivo de la persona que la usa.
> 4. Está ligada a la información o mensaje, de tal manera que si éstos son cambiados, la firma
>    digital es invalidada.
> 5. Está conforme a las reglamentaciones adoptadas por el Gobierno Nacional.

A digital signature (*firma digital*) is a specific figure: a cryptographic value bound to a
certificate issued to the signing person by a Digital Certification Entity, which gives rise to the
presumption of intent in article 28. The paragraph lists the attributes (unique to the person,
verifiable, under their exclusive control, bound to the message so that any change invalidates it,
compliant with government regulation) that give it the force of a handwritten signature.

## How bb-sign meets the conditions of the law

bb-sign provides electronic signature. The method is recorded in the evidence of every envelope:

- **Identification by email.** Each signer receives a personal link at their email address; only
  whoever controls that mailbox can open it.
- **Additional verification.** The organization can require, envelope by envelope, a one-time code
  sent to the same email, which the signer types before signing. Each signature records which of the
  two methods identified the signer.
- **Express approval.** The signer draws or types their signature, which is stamped into the document
  next to their name, email and the date.
- **Integrity.** Every signature is bound to the SHA-256 fingerprint of the documents the signer saw,
  so any later change is detected.
- **Evidence.** An audit trail records the date and time, IP address and browser of every action.
- **Evidence certificate.** When the envelope completes, bb-sign issues a sealed evidence certificate,
  for the organization, that brings that evidence together with
  a fixed copy of the audit trail. See [Evidence certificate](/en/sign/concepts/evidence-certificate/).

With this method, bb-sign meets both conditions of article 7: it identifies the signer and shows their
approval in a way the organization can explain and prove. See
[Electronic signature and seal](/en/sign/concepts/signature-and-seal/) and
[Evidence and audit](/en/sign/concepts/evidence-and-audit/).

## The organization's seal

When an envelope completes, bb-sign seals each document with the organization's certificate, and
includes in the seal the full certification chain the organization uploaded. The seal guarantees the
integrity of the document and shows which organization sealed it; each signer's identity is
established by the electronic-signature evidence described above.

Configure your organization's own certificate, issued by a Digital Certification Entity accredited by
ONAC, so that validators recognize the seal as trusted. Until you do, bb-sign seals with a Binary
Bridges testing certificate, and the app says so with the "Default testing certificate" notice. Before
the organization's own certificate expires, bb-sign emails the organization's admins (clause 2.4 of
the [Terms and conditions](/en/sign/legal/terms/)). See
[Your own certificate](/en/sign/admin/own-certificate/).

## Choosing the mechanism for each document

The law requires a method that is "reliable and appropriate for the purpose", and what is appropriate
depends on the document, the risk and the parties. That is why clause 1.2 of the
[Terms and conditions](/en/sign/legal/terms/) states that each customer, through its legal office,
decides whether the mechanism is appropriate for the documents it sends for signature. This page
gathers the facts for that decision.
