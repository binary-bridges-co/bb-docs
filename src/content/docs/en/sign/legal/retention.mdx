bb-sign backs every signed document with integrity, signer identification and evidence, and keeps it
for a fixed period. This page explains what the service covers, how long it keeps documents, how that
policy applies today and how your organization can archive each envelope to keep it for as long as
the law or your own rules require.

## What bb-sign covers

- **Integrity.** Every signed document is sealed, and any later change is detected.
- **Identification.** The signer is identified by their email and, when the organization requires
  it, by a one-time code. Each signature records the method used to identify the signer.
- **Record of what was signed.** Which documents each signer saw and signed is recorded with date
  and time, together with the SHA-256 hash of those documents.
- **Consistent evidence.** The audit chain lets you confirm that no record was inserted or removed.
- **Probative value.** When each envelope completes, bb-sign issues a sealed evidence certificate
  (who, what, when and from where) that the organization downloads.
- **Traceability.** Every relevant action on an envelope is recorded in its history.

bb-sign keeps documents for a fixed period set out in its terms. Once that period ends, keeping them
is the responsibility of the organization that sent them.

## Retention policy

These are the service's commitments, set out in section 3 of the
[Terms and conditions](/en/sign/legal/terms/). The
[Retention and restore guide](/en/sign/legal/retention-guide/) explains how to apply them.

| Item | Policy |
|---|---|
| Retention | The original documents, the signed documents and the evidence of each envelope are kept for <Fact of={RETENTION_POLICY.retentionDays} unit="days" />, counted from when each file is uploaded or generated. A fixed period, the same for every organization. When it ends, the file is deleted permanently. |
| Move to archive storage | Documents older than <Fact of={RETENTION_POLICY.archiveAfterDays} unit="days" /> may move to archive storage; they then cannot be downloaded immediately. |
| Restore | An authorized user of the organization, in the app or through the API, can request it, and the signer, from their link, can request it for their signed copy. When it finishes, bb-sign emails the people who asked and sends the `document.restored` webhook to subscribed integrations. It usually takes a few hours, with no guaranteed time. The restored copy stays available for <Fact of={RETENTION_POLICY.restoredCopyDays} unit="days" />. |
| Evidence certificate and audit trail | Never archived: they download immediately throughout the period. |
| After the period | Retention is the customer's responsibility (Ley 527 de 1999, article 12). |

## How the policy applies today

This table describes how the service behaves in production today.

| Item | Current behaviour |
|---|---|
| Document retention | A single rule: each file is deleted after <Fact of={RETENTION_IMPLEMENTED.documentsExpirationDays} unit="days" />. |
| Move to archive storage | No file moves to archive storage today, so every file downloads immediately throughout its period. |
| Restore on request | Available in the app, through the API and from the signer's link. While nothing is archived, there is no need to use it. |
| Envelope moves to "Archived" | <Fact of={ENVELOPE.archiveAfterDays} unit="days" /> after creation, a completed, cancelled or expired envelope moves to the "Archived" view. It is a change of list: the envelope and its files can still be downloaded until their retention period ends. |

The application's **Storage** page shows, separately, the product's declared policy and the rules the
storage actually applies. See [Storage](/en/sign/admin/storage/).

## What Ley 527 de 1999 says

Article 12 sets the conditions under which a retained data message satisfies a legal obligation to
retain. The Spanish text is the authoritative one:

> **Artículo 12. Conservación de los mensajes de datos y documentos.** Cuando la ley requiera que
> ciertos documentos, registros o informaciones sean conservados, ese requisito quedará satisfecho,
> siempre que se cumplan las siguientes condiciones:
>
> 1. Que la información que contengan sea accesible para su posterior consulta.
> 2. Que el mensaje de datos o el documento sea conservado en el formato en que se haya generado,
>    enviado o recibido o en algún formato que permita demostrar que reproduce con exactitud la
>    información generada, enviada o recibida, y
> 3. Que se conserve, de haber alguna, toda información que permita determinar el origen, el
>    destino del mensaje, la fecha y la hora en que fue enviado o recibido el mensaje o producido
>    el documento.
>
> No estará sujeta a la obligación de conservación, la información que tenga por única finalidad
> facilitar el envío o recepción de los mensajes de datos.
>
> Los libros y papeles del comerciante podrán ser conservados en cualquier medio técnico que
> garantice su reproducción exacta.

In summary: the information must remain accessible for later consultation; it must be kept in the
format in which it was generated, sent or received, or in one that demonstrably reproduces it
exactly; and any information that identifies its origin, destination, date and time must be kept
with it.

Article 13 allows that obligation to be met through third parties:

> **Artículo 13. Conservación de mensajes de datos y archivo de documentos a través de terceros.**
> El cumplimiento de la obligación de conservar documentos, registros o informaciones en mensajes de
> datos, se podrá realizar directamente o a través de terceros, siempre y cuando se cumplan las
> condiciones enunciadas en el artículo anterior.

In other words, retention may be carried out directly or through a third party, provided the
conditions of article 12 are met. bb-sign acts as that third party for the policy period. The sealed
PDF you download meets the second condition, because it reproduces exactly what was signed, and its
seal includes the name, email and date of every signature. To keep it for the years your obligation
requires, store it in your organization's archive.

## Archive every envelope on completion

The most reliable approach is automatic: when the `envelope.completed` event arrives, download the
signed copy of each document and the evidence certificate to your organization's archive, and check
that they are exactly what bb-sign sealed.

<Steps>

1. Create a webhook subscribed to `envelope.completed` and to `envelope.evidence_issued`, which tells
   you when the evidence certificate arrives later. See [Webhooks](/en/sign/developers/webhooks/).
2. Verify the signature of every delivery before processing it. If `data.signedDocumentsStatus` is
   `PREPARING`, the signed PDFs do not exist yet: try again later.
3. Call the path in `data.documentsPath` with a credential that holds `document:download` in the
   envelope's workspace (auditor role or above). Without that permission, `signedUrl` comes back
   empty.
4. Download every `signedUrl` and compare its SHA-256 with the `signedSha256` of the same document in
   `data.documents`. If they match, the file is exactly the one bb-sign sealed. The links are valid
   for <Fact of={ENVELOPE.downloadUrlSeconds} unit="seconds" />; download them as you receive them,
   without storing them.
5. Download the evidence certificate from `data.evidenceCertificate.downloadPath`, which returns a link
   valid for <Fact of={EVIDENCE.downloadLinkSeconds} unit="seconds" />, and compare its SHA-256 with
   `data.evidenceCertificate.sha256`.
6. Store `data.retention.deletesAt`: from that date bb-sign no longer has the files.

</Steps>

This code covers steps 2 to 4:

```bash
# event.json is the envelope.completed delivery, after its signature checks out.
if [ "$(jq -r '.data.signedDocumentsStatus // "READY"' event.json)" = "PREPARING" ]; then
  echo "Signed PDFs are still being prepared: try again later" >&2
  exit 75
fi
ENVELOPE_ID=$(jq -r '.data.envelope.id' event.json)
DOCUMENTS_PATH=$(jq -r '.data.documentsPath' event.json)
mkdir -p "archive/$ENVELOPE_ID"

curl -sSf -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" "$BBSIGN_BASE_URL$DOCUMENTS_PATH" \
  | jq -r '.[] | "\(.id)\t\(.signedUrl // "MISSING")\t\(.filename)"' \
  | while IFS=$'\t' read -r id url filename; do
      if [ "$url" = "MISSING" ]; then
        echo "No signed copy for $filename: the credential needs document:download" >&2
        exit 1
      fi
      curl -sSf -o "archive/$ENVELOPE_ID/$filename" "$url"
      expected=$(jq -r --arg id "$id" '.data.documents[] | select(.documentId == $id) | .signedSha256' event.json)
      actual=$(shasum -a 256 "archive/$ENVELOPE_ID/$filename" | cut -d ' ' -f 1)
      if [ "$actual" != "$expected" ]; then
        echo "SHA-256 of $filename does not match the webhook" >&2
        exit 1
      fi
    done
```

```js
import { createClient } from '@binarybridges/bb-sign-sdk'
import { createHash } from 'node:crypto'
import { mkdir, writeFile } from 'node:fs/promises'

const client = createClient({
  baseUrl: process.env.BBSIGN_BASE_URL,
  tokenUrl: process.env.BBSIGN_TOKEN_URL,
  clientId: process.env.BBSIGN_CLIENT_ID,
  clientSecret: process.env.BBSIGN_CLIENT_SECRET,
})

// Call this from your webhook handler with the data of an envelope.completed event whose signature checks out.
export async function archiveEnvelope(data) {
  if (data.signedDocumentsStatus === 'PREPARING') {
    throw new Error('Signed PDFs are still being prepared: try again later')
  }
  const envelopeId = data.envelope.id
  const expected = new Map((data.documents ?? []).map((d) => [d.documentId, d.signedSha256]))
  const documents = await client.envelopes.listEnvelopeDocuments({ id: envelopeId })
  await mkdir(`archive/${envelopeId}`, { recursive: true })

  for (const document of documents) {
    if (!document.signedUrl) {
      throw new Error(`No signed copy for ${document.filename}: the credential needs document:download`)
    }
    const response = await fetch(document.signedUrl)
    if (!response.ok) {
      throw new Error(`Download of ${document.filename} failed: HTTP ${response.status}`)
    }
    const bytes = Buffer.from(await response.arrayBuffer())
    if (createHash('sha256').update(bytes).digest('hex') !== expected.get(document.id)) {
      throw new Error(`SHA-256 of ${document.filename} does not match the webhook`)
    }
    await writeFile(`archive/${envelopeId}/${document.filename}`, bytes)
  }
}
```

```java
import co.binarybridges.sign.sdk.BbSignClient;
import co.binarybridges.sign.sdk.generated.model.DocumentWithUrlsResponse;
import java.net.URI;
import java.net.http.HttpClient;
import java.net.http.HttpRequest;
import java.net.http.HttpResponse;
import java.nio.file.Files;
import java.nio.file.Path;
import java.security.MessageDigest;
import java.util.HexFormat;
import java.util.List;
import java.util.Map;
import java.util.UUID;

// Call this from your webhook handler once the envelope.completed signature checks out,
// unless data.signedDocumentsStatus is PREPARING (then try again later).
// expectedSha256 maps each data.documents[].documentId to its signedSha256.
void archiveEnvelope(BbSignClient client, UUID envelopeId, Map<UUID, String> expectedSha256) throws Exception {
    List<DocumentWithUrlsResponse> documents =
            client.execute(client.envelopes().listEnvelopeDocuments(envelopeId));
    Path folder = Files.createDirectories(Path.of("archive", envelopeId.toString()));
    HttpClient http = HttpClient.newHttpClient();

    for (DocumentWithUrlsResponse document : documents) {
        if (document.getSignedUrl() == null) {
            throw new IllegalStateException("No signed copy for " + document.getFilename()
                    + ": the credential needs document:download");
        }
        HttpRequest request = HttpRequest.newBuilder(URI.create(document.getSignedUrl())).GET().build();
        HttpResponse<byte[]> response = http.send(request, HttpResponse.BodyHandlers.ofByteArray());
        if (response.statusCode() != 200) {
            throw new IllegalStateException("Download of " + document.getFilename()
                    + " failed: HTTP " + response.statusCode());
        }
        String actual = HexFormat.of().formatHex(MessageDigest.getInstance("SHA-256").digest(response.body()));
        if (!actual.equals(expectedSha256.get(document.getId()))) {
            throw new IllegalStateException("SHA-256 of " + document.getFilename() + " does not match the webhook");
        }
        Files.write(folder.resolve(document.getFilename()), response.body());
    }
}
```

  provide it from your organization's archive.
