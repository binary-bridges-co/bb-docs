The **envelope** is bb-sign's unit of work: it gathers one or more PDF documents, the list of signers
and the conditions of the sending (code verification, link validity, expiry date and signing
order). With it, you control the whole path of a signature, from draft to sealed documents. This
page describes that path, cancellation and expiry.

## Envelope statuses

<StatusFlow locale="en" of="envelope" />

The application shows these statuses as **DRAFT**, **SENT**, **IN_PROGRESS**, **COMPLETED**,
**CANCELLED** and **EXPIRED**, and lets you filter the envelope list by any of them.

- `IN_PROGRESS` appears on envelopes with more than one signer, from the first signature to the
  last. With a single signer, the envelope goes directly from `SENT` to `COMPLETED`.
- `COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED` and `EXPIRED` are final statuses.
- When the envelope completes, bb-sign seals every document with the organization's certificate.
  While it does, the detail shows "Signed PDF processing...". See
  [Electronic signature and seal](/en/sign/concepts/signature-and-seal/).

## Signer statuses

<StatusFlow locale="en" of="signer" />

Each signer moves forward independently. In the envelope detail, the **Signers** list shows each
one's status (**Pending**, **Notified**, **Viewed**, **Signed**) and their **Order #**. If a signer
is not going to sign, cancel the envelope or let it reach its expiry date.

## Sequential or parallel signing

When creating the envelope you choose one of two modes, which the envelope detail shows as
**Sequential signing** or **Parallel signing**:

| Mode | What happens on send | Order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parallel | Every signer is emailed at once and signs in any order. | The order number is informational. |
| Sequential | Signers are emailed one at a time, in the order set on the signers step. | Each signer gets their link once everyone before them has signed. |

In sequential mode, several signers can share the same order number: they form a group and are
emailed at the same time. When the group finishes, the next one receives the email
"It's your turn to sign". See [Signers and order](/en/sign/sending/signers-and-order/).

## Signing link validity

Each signer receives a unique link by email, valid for the hours set in **Link expiry (hours)** when
creating the envelope; by default, <Fact of={ENVELOPE.linkTtlHoursDefault} unit="hours" />. The
period starts when the link is generated: on send or, in sequential signing, when it becomes the
signer's turn.

Choose an expiry that gives each signer enough time, because an expired link is not resent. A link
expiring does not change the envelope's status, which stays in `SENT` or `IN_PROGRESS` until it
completes, is cancelled or expires.

## Expiry date

If you want the envelope to have a deadline, set a date in **Expires at (optional)**; if you leave it
empty, the envelope does not expire. bb-sign checks expiry dates every
<Fact of={ENVELOPE.expirySweepMinutes} unit="minutes" /> and marks as `EXPIRED` the envelopes in
`SENT` or `IN_PROGRESS` whose date has passed. Pending signers receive the email
"Signing request expired". The deadline counts from the moment of sending, so a draft does not
expire.

## Cancel an envelope

A Manager of the workspace or an administrator can cancel an envelope in `DRAFT`, `SENT` or
`IN_PROGRESS` with the **Cancel Envelope** button, after confirming. On cancellation, every pending
signing link is deactivated and the envelope stays in `CANCELLED` permanently.

bb-sign does not send a cancellation email to signers; if needed, let them know yourself. See
[Cancel an envelope](/en/sign/sending/cancel/).

## Send an envelope

To be sent, the envelope must be in `DRAFT` and have at least one signer and one document. On send,
bb-sign generates a unique signing link for each signer.

<Aside type="caution" title="Review the summary before sending">
  On send, the documents, the signer list and the signing mode are fixed, so that everyone signs
  exactly the same thing. If something needs to change afterwards, cancel the envelope and create a
  new one.
</Aside>

Labels can be changed in any envelope status. See [Labels](/en/sign/concepts/labels/).
