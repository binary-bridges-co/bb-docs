When an envelope completes, bb-sign issues its **evidence certificate**: a sealed PDF that gathers in
a single document what happened in the envelope. It presents its documents with their fingerprints,
its signers and how they were identified, the timeline and the integrity of its audit trail. bb-sign
stores it together with the envelope's **audit trail**, frozen as a JSON file, and neither of the two
changes afterwards.

It is what you hand over when you need to show how a document was signed. To understand the data it
summarizes, see [Evidence and audit](/en/sign/concepts/evidence-and-audit/).

## What it contains

| Block | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Header | The certificate identifier, the envelope and its title, the organization, the sender, the date it completed and which copy this is. |
| Documents | For each document: its name, the pages of the original, the original's SHA-256 fingerprint and how it was computed, the signed document's SHA-256 fingerprint and the upload date. |
| Signers | For each signer: name, email, order, identification method, the one-time code if one was required (channel, masked destination and the time it was accepted), IP address, browser, when they were invited, opened the envelope and signed, the fingerprint of the document set they signed, and their seal's hash and version. |
| Agreements | Today it always reads "No agreements attached". |
| Seal | The subject, issuer, serial number, fingerprint and validity of the certificate that sealed, how many certificates its chain holds and whether it is the organization's or the testing one. |
| Timeline | The events of the audit trail, with their actor and hash. |
| Integrity | The head of the audit chain (the last event it covers, with its hash), the coverage of that chain and the SHA-256 fingerprint of the audit trail. |
| Retention | Until when each file of the envelope is kept. |
| How to verify | The steps to check the seal, the documents and the audit trail. |

If the envelope was sealed with bb-sign's testing certificate, the **Seal** block warns about it. If
part of the audit trail was recorded under an older scheme, the **Integrity** block says so and
explains what the chain proves for those events.

## Organization copy and signer copy

Every certificate has two copies:

| Copy | Who downloads it | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organization | The users and API credentials that can download the envelope's documents (Auditor role or higher in its workspace). | Everything the certificate records, together with the audit trail as JSON. |
| Signer | Each signer, from their signing link. | The same envelope, with every email masked and no IP addresses, browsers or one-time code destinations.{!EVIDENCE.signerCopyIncludesAuditTrail.value && ' It does not include the audit trail; if a signer needs it, the organization can provide it.'} |

## Language

The certificate is issued in the organization's language: Spanish, the default, or English. Binary
Bridges sets that language when it configures the organization. Each certificate keeps the language
it was issued in; if you need to change it for future certificates, ask Binary Bridges.

## When it is issued

bb-sign issues the certificate after the envelope completes and its documents are sealed{EVIDENCE.usuallyIssuedWithinMinutes.value && ', usually within a few minutes'}. Meanwhile, the envelope detail shows "Evidence certificate processing...", and
the signing page shows the signer "Preparing your evidence certificate".

The "Signing complete" email signers receive waits for the certificate for at most
<Fact of={EVIDENCE.degradedAfterHours} unit="hours" />. If the certificate is not ready by then, the
email goes out anyway and says it is being prepared, at the same link. Integrations receive the
`envelope.completed` webhook with the certificate's status and, if it arrives later,
`envelope.evidence_issued`. See [Webhooks](/en/sign/developers/webhooks/).

Binary Bridges can issue a certificate that failed again at most <Fact of={EVIDENCE.reissueMax} />
times, and only within <Fact of={EVIDENCE.reissueWindowDays} unit="days" /> of the failure and while the
envelope has not moved to **Archived**. Each new issue is recorded in the envelope's audit trail.

## How to download it

**From the envelope detail.** Once the certificate is ready, the **Evidence certificate** block offers
two downloads: **Evidence certificate (PDF)**, the organization copy, and **Audit trail (JSON)**.
Below them, "SHA-256 of the certificate:" shows the PDF's fingerprint. Whoever cannot download the
envelope's documents sees "The evidence certificate is available to those who can download this
envelope's documents."; ask an administrator for the Auditor role in that workspace.

Each download creates links valid for <Fact of={EVIDENCE.downloadLinkMinutes} unit="minutes" /> and is
recorded in the audit trail as `EVIDENCE_CERTIFICATE_ACCESSED`. If a link expires, press the download
again. Envelopes that have moved to **Archived** keep both downloads.

**From the signer's link.** When the envelope completes, the signing page offers the signer
**Download Your Evidence Certificate**, with their copy. The link keeps serving the copy after the
envelope moves to Archived. See [Sign and download](/en/sign/signing/sign-and-download/).

**Through the API.** Integrations read the certificate's status and get the download links with the
[getEvidenceCertificate](/sign/api/operations/getevidencecertificate/) and
[downloadEvidenceCertificate](/sign/api/operations/downloadevidencecertificate/) operations, with the
same permission as the documents.

## How long it is kept

bb-sign keeps the certificate, the signer copy and the audit trail for
<Fact of={EVIDENCE.retentionDays} unit="days" /> from issue, protected against deletion and modification for that period. They are never
moved to archive storage, so they download directly for the whole period. At the end, the detail
shows "The retention period has ended and the certificate was deleted.".

<Aside type="caution" title="Keep your copy">
  If you must keep the evidence for longer, download the certificate and the audit trail before the
  period ends and store them in your own systems. See [Scope and retention](/en/sign/legal/retention/).
</Aside>

## Envelopes completed before evidence certificates

Envelopes that completed before bb-sign started issuing evidence certificates show "Completed before
evidence certificates were enabled." and will never have one. Their evidence remains available in the
**Audit Trail** of the envelope detail, and their documents download as usual during their retention
period.

## Check what you received

1. **The file.** Compute the SHA-256 fingerprint of the downloaded PDF (for example, with
   `shasum -a 256`) and compare it with "SHA-256 of the certificate:" in the envelope detail.
2. **The seal.** Open the PDF in Adobe Acrobat or another reader that shows signatures: the seal
   covers the whole file, and any change invalidates it.
3. **The documents.** Compare each signed document's fingerprint with the one the **Documents** block
   prints, or check it on the public page with **Check a file**. See
   [Verify a signed document](/en/sign/signing/verify-a-document/).
