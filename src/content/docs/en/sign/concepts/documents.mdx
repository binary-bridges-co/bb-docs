An envelope contains one or more documents, and every signer signs all of them. You decide where on
each document each signer's seal appears. This page explains which files bb-sign accepts, how they
are handled while you prepare the envelope and how to place the seals.

## Format and size

bb-sign works with documents in <Fact of={DOCUMENTS.contentType} /> format. If you have a document in
Word, as an image or in another format, convert it to PDF before uploading it.

Each file can weigh up to <Fact of={DOCUMENTS.maxUploadMb} unit="MB" />. If a file is larger, the
application shows "That file is too large."; shrink the PDF (for example, by lowering the resolution
of its images) and upload it again.

An envelope needs at least one document to be sent.

## Documents uploaded before sending

The wizard uploads documents in its second step, before the envelope is created. Until you press
**Send Envelope**, those files are **unassigned**: they belong to your organization, but not yet to
an envelope.

- **They do not consume quota.** Storage is charged when the envelope is created. See
  [Quotas](/en/sign/concepts/quotas/).
- **They have a cap per organization.** Across all users, unassigned documents can add up to
  <Fact of={DOCUMENTS.maxUnassignedPerOrgMb} unit="MB" />. When the cap is reached, the upload shows
  "Your organization has run out of document storage. Ask your administrator to raise the limit.";
  finish or discard pending wizards to free up space.
- **They are removed automatically** after <Fact of={DOCUMENTS.unassignedRetentionHours} unit="hours" />.
  If you resume a wizard after that, the application asks you to upload the PDFs again; the title,
  signers and options are kept.

Each document belongs to one envelope only. If the wizard says a document is already part of another
envelope, remove it and upload it again.

## Place each signer's seal

When the envelope completes, bb-sign stamps on every document one seal per signer, with their name,
email, date, signature, hash and QR code (see
[Electronic signature and seal](/en/sign/concepts/signature-and-seal/)). You choose where each one
goes.

### With a marker

Write in the PDF, where you want the seal, the text <Fact of={DOCUMENTS.placementMarker} />,
replacing `N` with the signer's order number: `{{signer1}}` for the first, `{{signer2}}` for the
second, and so on. You can use any font, as long as the PDF contains the marker as text and not as
an image. When sealing, bb-sign removes the marker and places the seal with its top-left corner where
the marker began.

bb-sign looks for each marker on every page. Signers with a marker get their seal at their marker,
and the others at the default position.

<Aside type="tip" title="Leave room under the marker">
  The seal is drawn downwards from the marker's line and takes the width of a signature with its
  details. Put the marker on a line of its own, with several blank lines below it, and test your
  template with a single-signer envelope before using it with customers.
</Aside>

### Without a marker

If the document has no marker for a signer, their seal goes to the **bottom-right corner of the
last page**. With several signers without markers, the seals stack upwards in that corner, in signer
order. If they do not fit on the last page, bb-sign adds a page at the end and continues there.

## Original and signed document

In the envelope detail, every document offers two downloads: **Original PDF**, the file as uploaded,
and **Signed PDF**, the same file with the seals stamped and the organization's certificate
signature. The signed document is available once the envelope is `COMPLETED`; while it is being
generated, the detail shows "Signed PDF processing...".

Downloading documents requires the Auditor role or higher in the workspace; a Viewer sees their
names. See [Download signed documents](/en/sign/sending/download-signed-documents/).

The signed document corresponds exactly to the PDF you attached when sending the envelope.
